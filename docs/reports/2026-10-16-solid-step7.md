# Solid: step 7, copies and versions of the library

The step fixed the findings of step 7 of "Fix steps" in
`docs/audit/summary.md`: S318, M338 and M366, with decision 10 of
"Decisions of 2026-10-11". It ran in one session on 2026-10-16. The logs
lie in `build/drive/logs/`, under names that start with `step7-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Decision 10 | `93ced37c` | `Changed` stood in `anti.collection.pool` and `Snapshot` in `anti.collection.concurrent`, so each kind of thread-safe collection imported a sibling module for one item. |
| S318, `Snapshot` | `5518e342` | The walk held its room through a pointer that is not `own` and replaced no `copy`, so `dup` gave two walks that tore down one room and its elements. |
| S318, `NotOne` and `Changed` | `0d126d62` | `dup` copied the `str` of the message as a pointer, so a copy read bytes its original freed. |
| S318, `ArenaAllocator` | `f223467d` | `dup` copied the head of the list of blocks, so the copy and the original gave every block back. |
| S318, `FileSink` | `a0de8b0c` | `dup` copied the pointer to the open file, so the copy wrote to a file its original had closed and closed it again. |
| M338 | `9c1100dd` | `ConcurrentMap` stamped `e.0` inside the change, after the change could rewrite it, so the entry kept its old version. |
| M366 | `d4a0c9dc` | `make_room` asked for room for `count + 1`, which a full room with one removed entry already holds, so it rebuilt at the same size on every `add`. |

`1cdae3ba` writes the commits into the rows of the summary.

- Decision 10. `Changed` and `Snapshot` stand in `anti.collection` beside
  `NotOne`. Every collection module imports it. The specifications, the
  overview, chapter 14 of the guide and the tests name them there.
- S318. Each class got a failing test first: `std_snapshot_dup` crashed,
  `std_error_copies` printed the message of another error and its run
  under `--memory-checks` reported a heap use after free,
  `std_arena_dup` aborted on the double free, and `std_file_sink_dup`
  crashed and reported a use after free under `--memory-checks`. The logs
  are `step7-red-s318-*.log`. The language has no refusal of `dup` for a
  class, so every one got a copy. `Snapshot` copies its room and its
  elements. `ArenaAllocator` gives an empty allocator over the same
  `from`. `NotOne`, `Changed` and `FileSink` share their bytes or their
  file with every copy through `anti.mem.Shared`, since an error cannot
  replace `copy` without the private fields of `lang.Error`, and the hook
  `copied` is dropped under `--no-hooks`.
- M338. `std_map_key_rewrite` rewrites a key through `update_all` and
  `update_first` of both maps. `ConcurrentMap` kept the old version and
  let the old version write, `step7-red-m338.log`. It now copies the key
  before the change, as `SyncMap` does.
- M366. `std_map_growth` gave capacity 8 where 16 is due for a `Map` and
  a `Set`, `step7-red-m366.log`. The DESIGN comment and the entry of
  `docs/decisions.md` say "at the same size" where they said "in place",
  since a rebuild takes a new block.
