# Sum types

"Sum types" of `docs/anti-language-additions.md` is built: `variant` declarations with
cases and fields, the tag and the union of C, the literals of the cases, `switch` that
binds the fields of a case and covers every case, `if let`, `v is Shape.Circle`, `v.tag`
and the enum of the tags in the C header. The development Mac passes 668 of 668 tests,
and the ASan and UBSan builds pass 667 each, without `no_paths`. The build has no
warnings. The VMs did not run.

## What was done

1. `0176c96`. `variant` is a keyword. A variant is `TYPE_VARIANT`, a struct of the field
   `tag`, an enum over the smallest unsigned integer that holds the number of cases, and
   the union `u` of one struct per case that has fields. The passes after the checker see a
   struct, so layout, copying and the ABI are the struct's, and `packed` and `align(N)`
   apply. `Shape.Empty` becomes the literal `Shape.Empty { }` in the checker. `switch`
   reads the tag once, and an arm that binds copies the fields of its case before the
   body. `if let` is a switch of one arm and an `else`. The tag is read-only, `v.u` is
   refused, and `fallthrough` never enters an arm that binds.
2. `c9c61b9`. The library file writes a variant as the struct C sees, and the reader
   derives the cases from the enum and the union. The format is version 44. Another module
   writes `geo.Shape.Circle { }`, `geo.Shape.Empty` and `v is geo.Shape.Circle`. The header
   writes `enum Shape_tag` and the typedef with `uint8_t tag` and the union `u`.
3. `c18dd1b`. A unit test pins the tag width at 255 and 256 cases. The manifest of
   `emit_identity` gains the six files of `programs/variants` and changes no other line.
4. `919b2ea`. The decisions gain "Sum types", `docs/notes/variants.md` holds the choices of
   the passes, and the overview, the specification and CLAUDE.md follow.
5. `3bbacc1`. `p is Shape.Circle` on a pointer names the pointer in its message.
6. The tests. `tests/unit/test_variant.c` holds the lexer, three parse trees, 4 accepted
   and 31 refused sources, exhaustiveness among them, and the tag width.
   `programs/variants` runs on ARM64, under Rosetta on x86_64 and in dev mode: sizes,
   literals, bindings, `if let`, `is`, `tag`, fields, elements, parameters, results, a
   pointer, `fallthrough` and a variant inside a case.
   `variant_modules_release` and `variant_modules_dev` take a variant across a module.
   `clib_variants` compares the header with `tests/dump/variants.h`, and C builds, passes
   and reads variants through it, with a C++17 check. `listing_error_variant_cover` pins
   the coverage messages at the command line.

## What failed and how it was fixed

- A `sed` that dropped `field.vis = VIS_PUB;` from the parser of a case removed the same
  line from the parser of a class field. Unit checks of the parser, the checker and
  lowering failed on `pub` fields, and the line is back.
- The reader derived the cases of a variant before its union had fields, since the union
  follows the variant in the table. A pass after the fields derives them now.
- The library format moved to 44. `test_modules.c` and `antl_scale` hold its bytes, and
  only the version changed. `errors/switch_str.err` names variants among the types a
  `switch` takes.
- The tag width test passed at once. A mutation of `<=` to `<` made it fail, and it was
  reverted.
- The host link prints `ignoring -lto_library`, as in every earlier session.

## Provisional entries

Under "Sum types" in `docs/decisions.md`: the numbering of the tags and the name
`Shape.tag`, no union member for a case without fields, no empty variant, the fields of a
case, `enum Shape_tag` and `Shape_Circle` in the header, `packed` over the cases, the copy

names of another module.

## Questions

- The access sentence of the specification named `switch` alone, and the overview and
  the task wrote `if let`. The sentence now names both. Is that the intended reading?
- The decisions say that `anti.reflect.Value` becomes a variant with the same layout once
  `variant` exists. The layout matches: a `u8` tag of eight cases and a union at offset 8.
  The change removes `kind` and `data` from the API of `anti.reflect` and its callers, so
  it was left for a step of its own. Should it be the next one?
