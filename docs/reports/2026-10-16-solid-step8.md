# Solid: step 8, `anti.log` and `anti.args`

The step fixed the findings of step 8 of "Fix steps" in
`docs/audit/summary.md`: S313, S317, S332 and the minor finding on the
forms of an option. It built decision 12 of "Decisions of 2026-10-11" as
well. It ran
in one session on 2026-10-16. The logs lie in `build/drive/logs/`, under
names that start with `step8-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| S313 | `d385f56c` | `construct` of `Log` looked a registered kind up through `Log.get()`, which found no instance yet and built a second `Log`, so the file was read again until the stack ran out. |
| S317 | `ba344114` | Fixed by step 3. `reserve` gave up without a word when `alloc` gave nothing, and `declare` wrote past the slice. |
| S332 | `fd716910` | `first_sink` made the sink of the first entry it could, read no `level` of an entry and stopped at the eighth. |
| Decision 12 | `46e8d189` | `text_of` and `at` gave `""` for a name or an index that holds nothing. |
| Forms of an option | `fc960111` | `read_option` dropped the value of a flag, the header left out `-n=value`, and `is_option` took `-5` for an option. |

`5b3b1b84` writes the commits into the rows of the summary, and
`cfafb900` takes the wrapping of the new doc comments from `anti fmt`.

- S313. `conf_logger_unknown_kind` names the kind `syslog`, and
  `conf_logger_register` registers a kind before the first line. Both
  ended with a segmentation fault, `step8-t1.log`. The kinds now stand
  in the private singleton `Kinds`, whose `construct` reads nothing.
- S317. `std_out_of_memory` refuses the `args.anti` of `ba344114^` with
  "2 of 2 alloc(T, n) without the stop" and passes on today's,
  `step8-oom-old.log` and `step8-oom-new.log`.
- S332. `conf_logger_sinks` gave every line to the first sink alone,
  `conf_logger_ninth_sink` never reached the ninth entry, and
  `conf_logger_unopened` wrote a second line, "no sink of kind file",
  `step8-t3.log`. The logger of a file is now the private `FileLogger`,
  which writes each line to the sink of every entry whose level takes it.
  The header names the key `name`, which the code read already.
- Decision 12. `std_args_absent` printed two empty texts where `none`
  belongs. `int_of` gives its fallback for an undeclared name.
- Forms of an option. `std_args_flag_value` and `std_args_number` failed,
  and `std_args_letter_value` pins `-n=value`, which worked already.

## The freeze

The step adds three public items: `UNKNOWN_OPTION`, `MISSING_VALUE` and
`FLAG_WITH_VALUE` of `anti.args`. The minor finding names the bare codes
1 and 2 of `parse` as the defect, and a program cannot test the code of a
refusal by name without a constant, as it does with those of
`anti.reflect` and `anti.plugin`. A flag given a value needed a third
code. `Kinds`, `FileLogger` and `Leg` of `anti.log` are private.
