# Solid: step 29, the file headers of the front end

Step 29 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: the file headers and
the comments of public headers, M346 to M350 and M365, in six sessions,
one per directory. This first session took the front end of antic: the
files of `lexer`, `parser`, `ast`, `types`, `sema`, `pattern`,
`diagnostic`, `warnings` and `modpath`. The logs are in
`build/drive/logs/s29/`.

## Findings closed for the front end

| Finding | Commit | Cause and fix |
|---|---|---|
| M346, files without the sentence of their purpose | `5ee03303` | Sixteen files opened with includes, a DESIGN or the comment of one item, four headers kept their sentence below the includes, and `sema_hash.c` had two comments stacked as one header. Each now opens with the sentence of `docs/notes/source-tree.md`. The commit message says twenty files, and 21 is the count. |
| M348, public functions that share a comment or have none | `5ee03303` | 24 declarations of `types.h`, `diagnostic.h`, `warnings.h`, `modpath.h`, `ast.h` and `sema.h` had no comment of their own. Each now says what it takes, gives and refuses, read from its definition: `types_set_fields` copies and overwrites every `home`, `types_struct` gives a distinct type per call, `types_is_integer` leaves out `char`, `bool` and an enum. |
| Headers that say less than their file, front end | `5ee03303` | `parser_decl.c` named the fields that `parser_item.c` reads, and `sema_expr.c`, `sema_call.c`, `sema_stmt.c`, `sema_class.c`, `sema_const.c`, `sema_pattern.c` and the `sema.c` line of `sema_checker.h` left out what they hold. Each now names it. |

The test `file_headers` refuses a file of the front end that opens with no
comment, with a DESIGN, PERF DECISION or `[AI AGENT]` comment, or with a
comment fastened to the item below it. It also refuses a function of a
public header without a comment of its own. It failed with 45 findings
before the fix, `fh1.log`. `file_headers_runner` shows each refusal on
made-up trees, `fhself.log`. The shared headers `parser_parser.h` and
`sema_checker.h` are outside the second rule, since `header_sections`
checks them. The summary records both findings as fixed for the front
end, `e1eeddb0`.
