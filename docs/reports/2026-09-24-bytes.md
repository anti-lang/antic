# Bytes

The step built "Bytes" of `docs/anti-language-additions.md`: `ByteRegex`, the mode taken
from where a literal is used, the byte methods, the class rules of byte patterns,
`to_bytes` and `to_text`, and `patch` with a byte sequence or a pattern, `into`, `at`,
`limit` and the fit rule.

## What was done

- `ByteRegex` is a struct of `anti.lang` beside `Regex`, and the match of a byte pattern
  is `ByteMatch` or `?ByteMatch`, whose texts are `[]byte`. A pattern literal checked
  where a `ByteRegex` is expected compiles in byte mode. `ByteRegex.compile` calls
  `regex.compile_bytes`.
- `anti_rt_regex_compile_bytes` of `src/rt/regex.c` compiles without UTF mode, with
  `PCRE2_NEVER_UTF`, `PCRE2_NEVER_UCP` and `PCRE2_DOTALL`. A character outside ASCII
  becomes an atom of its bytes, and a class that lists one a choice. A negated class or
  a range with one is refused at the character, and the message names text mode.
  antic and the runtime share the code.
- `matches`, `find_all`, `replace` and `split` of `[]byte` call the `bytes_` functions
  of `anti.regex`, with `regex.ByteMatches` and `regex.BytePieces` as the iterators.
- `data.patch(find, with, into, at, limit)` calls `patch_bytes`, `patch_bytes_fixed`,
  `patch`, `patch_literal` or `patch_fixed`. With literal operands the checker checks the
  fit, for a pattern against the fewest bytes its group can match
  (`pattern_least_bytes` of `src/antic/pattern.c`). Otherwise the call fails with the new
  `regex.LengthMismatch`, before any byte changes.
- `s.to_bytes()` and `data.to_text()` are calls of `anti.text` that copy, and `to_text`
  fails on bytes that are no valid UTF-8.
- The library format is 60.
- Tests: `programs/pattern_bytes.anti` and `programs/pattern_patch.anti` in release and
  dev mode, and the listing `pattern_bytes` of `tests/errors/`. The listing holds a class
  with a character outside ASCII (`[óa]`, also in the program), a refused negated class, a
  refused range, `(*UCP)`, the two wrong pattern types and `patch` refusing a `with` that
  does not fit a byte sequence, a group of a literal, the offset `at` and the whole match.

## What failed and how it was fixed

- `none` of a `ByteMatch` stored a `[]byte` field as one aggregate, which the back end
  cannot select. `lower_build_into` now zeroes a slice field in two words, as a `str`.
- `rt_plugin_tests` and `rt_plugin_table_tests` compile `src/rt/text.c` alone, and the
  UTF-8 check of `to_text` needs `src/rt/utf.c`, which both now compile.
- Messages longer than 111 bytes were cut in a diagnostic, so the two refusals are
  shorter.
- The first full run (`build/drive/logs/bytes-suite1.log`) failed five tests: the
  version pins of `tests/unit/test_modules.c` and `tests/modules/scale.antl.hex`,
  `fmt_canonical` and `anti_check` on four sources `anti fmt` then rewrapped,
  `link_identity_macos-arm64`, since the runtime grew, and `emit_identity`. Both
  digests were written on the Mac. The manifest changed for the two new programs and
  for the ten programs that hold `anti.regex` or `anti.text`.
- The docs-style checker reads `tests/CMakeLists.txt` as Markdown, and reports 250
  findings there before this step and 260 after it. Each finding is a `#` comment read
  as a heading, in the style the whole file has. They are left as they are. Every other
  touched file reports nothing.

## Provisional entries added

All stand under "Regular expressions" in `docs/decisions.md`: `ByteRegex` and
`ByteMatch`, the options of a byte pattern with `.` matching the newline, a character
outside ASCII as an atom and a class as a choice, byte escapes allowed in negated classes
and ranges with the two messages, the step past an empty match, the `bytes_` functions
and the two classes, `patch` by position, the fit measured on the fewest bytes of a group,
a computed `into` as a computed operand, the two passes of `patch`, `MissingGroup` and the
stop for `into`, places without overlap and the empty sequence, `LengthMismatch` in
`anti.regex`, and `to_bytes` and `to_text` in `anti.text`.
