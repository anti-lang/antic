# Step 34, rules 18 and 19, first session

Step 34 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` takes rules 18 and 19
of `docs/c-guidelines.md` last, one file per session. This session took
the first file the step names, `sema_stmt.c`. The logs stand in
`build/drive/logs/` under the prefix `s34-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Rule 19, `sema_stmt.c` past 3000 lines (front-end, tool-pass) | `1c09e047` | The file held the anonymous functions and the fields a `construct` sets beside the statements, and stood at 3511 lines. |

The defect was confirmed by its count: 3511 lines at `fb252385`, past
the 3089 of the audit. The anonymous functions and closures moved to the
new `sema_closure.c`, 509 lines. They leave 3010 lines, still past the
threshold, so the definite assignment of `construct`, the second split
the front-end report names, moved into `sema_class.c` beside the rest of
`construct`. `sema_stmt.c` stands at 2707 lines and `sema_class.c` at
1833.

The move changes no line of code. Six helpers the files share now take
the prefix `sema_`: `sema_block_returns`, `sema_declared_result`,
`sema_calls_never`, `sema_held_deepest`, `sema_check_closure_lifetime`
and `sema_check_construct_sets`. A comparison of every line of the old
files with the new ones, the renames undone, finds only the header
comments, the includes and the rewrapped signatures.

The test of the finding is the suite. Rule 18 says the threshold is a
reason to look and not a rule to enforce, so no test counts lines. The
suite holds the behaviour of every moved check, and `header_sections`
and `source_tree` refuse `sema_closure.c` without its section in
`sema_checker.h`, its name at the head of the header and its row in
`docs/notes/source-tree.md`. `source_tree` failed until the row stood
and the file was tracked.

`8702f47c` records the commit in the row of rule 19 of the summary.
