# The sweep of the paths of a function

The step did C3 of "Compile-time checks of 2026-10-20" in
`docs/decisions.md`: the sweep of the paths of every kind of function and
handler. Logs are under `build/drive/logs/checks-c3/`.

## The sweep

About 150 probe programs, `probe1.log` to `probe8.log`, took each kind
through each way a path goes on. The kinds were a function with a result,
a `may fail` function, a function that returns `never`, the handler of a
value and the `else` of a `let`. Each kind stood in a function, a method,
an operator, a worker, a generic, a nested type, a closure, a snapshot and
a `tests` block. The handler of a value stood in a `let`, an assignment, a
compound assignment, an argument, an operand, a cast, a literal, an index,
a field, a `return`, a `yield`, a `fail`, a `send`, an `assert`, a
condition, the value of a `switch`, a bound of a `for` and after `?.`. A
function value that reaches its end was converted to a function type that
returns `never`, in ten forms.

The checker refused every path that ends without what its kind requires.
No gap of that kind was found. Each accepted probe was compiled to the end,
`full-compile2.log`, and one of them, a `break` in a handler of a loop
condition, showed T189.

The tests now hold the grid. Forty refusals
`tests/matrix/flow_paths_<kind>_after_<way>` take the five kinds through a
`switch` with an arm that reaches its end, a loop, a `defer`, an `undo`, a
call statement with a handler, a `let` with an `else`, an `if` nested deep
and a `while true` whose `break` hides in a `let`. Each holds one message.
`flow_paths_that_end_nested` runs the forms that end, nested and combined,
in all three modes.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| T189 | `72c8c307` | The checker reads the condition of a `while` and of a `do ... while` inside its loop, and `lower_loop` wrote it outside, so a `break` that ends a handler there crashed antic or left the outer loop. |

## Findings recorded and left

- T190, minor, `fmt_handler_in_parentheses`. `anti fmt` indents the
  statements of a handler inside parentheses after the first as
  continuation lines. It belongs to the step of the formatter. Until then
  `fmt_canonical` passes over the fmt case and over
  `matrix/flow_exit_from_a_handler_in_a_loop_condition`.

## Not findings

A function with a result whose last statement is a `try` block, a
`do ... while` or a `select` that ends the path on every way is refused.
The entry on missing returns in `docs/decisions.md` and R003 name the forms
that end a path, and these three are not among them. The checker keeps the
documented rule. A question for Eddie: should these three forms end the
path as a `switch` does?

## Provisional entries added

One, after the entries of C2: a `break` or a `continue` that ends a handler
in the condition of a `while` or of a `do ... while` belongs to that loop.
`continue` tests the condition again. A bound and a step of a `for` belong
to the loop around it, as the checker already read them.
