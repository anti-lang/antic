# The safety check escaping-local

The step built E1 of "Compile-time checks of 2026-10-20" in
`docs/decisions.md`. Logs are under `build/drive/logs/checks-e1/`.

## What changed

Commit `c29e8289` builds the check. `src/antic/sema_escape.c` walks every
function body after the checker and follows each reference to a local or
a parameter. It refuses one that is returned, yielded out of its handler,
stored into a local of an outer block, into a place a parameter or `self`
reaches, into a static field or into a collection of an outer block. It
refuses one passed to `dispatch` or `parallel`, and one captured by a
closure at a `concurrent` parameter. The tests came first and failed,
`red.log`: 19 refusals and one program of the allowed uses in
`tests/matrix/ownership_*`, and `tests/errors/escaping_local` for a dev
build and `unchecked`. The ownership chapter of the guide describes the
check, and the note of warnings and the overview list it as built.

Commit `e92744d3` marks the places of the repository that the check
refuses with `unchecked(escaping-local, "until the step sweep")`, 107 in
`tests/` and 10 in the guide. 93 pass the slice of a local array to
`parallel`, 23 pass the address of a local to `dispatch`, and one stores
the address of a local into a static field. `tools/fuzz/generate.cmake`
writes the clause before each `parallel` of a local array. The hidden
context of the threads example of the overview takes its slice from
`alloc`, so the text a reader sees did not change.

## Mistakes of the task

- An array does not become a slice at `return`, so "a slice of a local
  array" is a slice expression alone. The test of that form was dropped
  before it was committed.
- A channel holds pointer-free values, so `send` of a reference fails its
  type first, and E1 has no message for it.
- The rules of closures refuse a closure by reference that is returned or
  stored, and a snapshot holds no pointer. Of the closure cases, the
  `concurrent` parameter alone reaches E1.

## Question for Eddie

`parallel` waits for every chunk before it returns, so the slice of a
local array it takes never outlives the array. The rule refuses it as
written, which is 93 of the 117 marked places and the usual form of the
examples of `parallel` in the guide. Should `parallel` stay on the list of
E1, or leave it?
