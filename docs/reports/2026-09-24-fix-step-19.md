# Fix step 19, the runtime on Windows

Branch `fix/runtime`, commits `354e90c` and `dd16830`. Logs are under
`build/drive/logs/`, named `s19-*`.

## Findings

- M20. Fixed. `anti_rt_getenv` of `src/rt/platform_windows.c` reads
  `ANTI_CONF` with `GetEnvironmentVariableW` into a buffer of the length
  it reports and converts it to UTF-8. `anti_rt_library_open` loads a
  plugin with `LoadLibraryW` from its UTF-8 path.
- M27. Fixed. `anti_rt_sleep` on Windows is a loop of `Sleep` steps of at
  most a day. `anti_rt_sleep_step` of `platform.h` sizes each step and
  rounds it up to the millisecond.
- M29. Fixed for the files of this step. `conf.c` and `plugin.c` hold no
  `#if` on the system any more, and `time.c` and `platform.c` moved whole
  into `platform_posix.c` and `platform_windows.c`. The branches of
  `trace.c`, `fs.c`, `threads.c`, `cpu.c`, `signal.c`, `mem.c`, `sync.c`,
  `start.c`, `loaded.c`, `errno.c` and `atomic.c`, and those of
  `src/antic/` and `src/anti/`, are left. They lie outside the files the
  step names.

## Tests

The defects of M20 and M27 show on Windows alone, and this session ran on
the Mac. The new tests fail there before the fix and pass on every host
after it.

- `rt_platform`, `tests/unit/test_platform.c`. It checks the sleep steps
  at 2^32 and 2^32 - 1 milliseconds and at `INT64_MAX`, and an
  environment value of 5000 bytes and one outside ASCII. It also checks
  the shared path rules and a library opened from a directory named
  `platform-ü`. It failed to link before the layer existed
  (`s19-build-red.log`).
- `conf_environment_long` gives `ANTI_CONF` a value past 1023 bytes, and
  `conf_environment_utf8` gives it a path outside ASCII. Both passed on
  the Mac before the fix (`s19-ctest-red-conf.log`), as the finding
  expects.

The Windows file compiles for `windows-x86_64` and `windows-arm64` at
every level under `-Werror` in the cross runtime of the host build.

## Outside the listed files

- `CMakeLists.txt`. The list of runtime sources names `platform_posix`
  and `platform_windows` in place of `platform` and `time`, and
  `platform.h` joins the dependencies of each object. The new files do
  not build without it.
- `tests/link-identity/return42.macos-arm64.sha256`. The runtime changed,
  so the link of `return42` did.

`enum anti_rt_lock`, and the rounding of a sleep on Windows.
