# Fix step 28, anti platform layer

Step 28 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M30 and M31 for
`anti`. `src/anti/platform.c` and `platform.h` are now the platform
layer of anti that rule 22 names. `files.c`, `sdk.c` and `syms.c` call
it, and every file of `src/anti/` includes `platform.h` by its name
alone. Logs are in `build/drive/logs/s28-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M30, anti | Fixed. The 11 host `#if` lines of `src/anti/` are gone: 6 in `files.c`, 3 in `sdk.c` and 2 in `syms.c`. The layer holds `platform_kind`, which says what a path names with or without following a link, `platform_make_dir`, `platform_remove_entry`, `platform_rename`, `platform_copy_permissions` and `platform_run_symbolized`. The walk and the removal of a tree stand on them, with the listing of `platform_list_directory` through `files_list_names`, which `copy_stubs` of `sdk.c` uses too. `anti sdk export` asks `target_host` whether it runs on a Mac. `syms.c` reads `ASAN_OPTIONS` with `platform_getenv`. |
| M31, anti | Fixed. The layer converts with `platform_widen`, which the layer of antic now exports under one `#if` of its header, and calls `_wmkdir`, `GetFileAttributesW`, `CreateFileW`, `DeleteFileW`, `RemoveDirectoryW` and `MoveFileExW`. `main` of anti reads its arguments through `platform_arguments`. The calls of `remove` in `build.c` and `repo.c` and of `rename` in `sdk.c` go through the layer. |

Two changes of behaviour follow from one code path for every host. On
Windows the walk now passes over a link to a file that leads nowhere.
The entry under "Build tool and distribution" already says so. On
POSIX an entry that disappears between the listing and its check is
passed over, where it failed the walk before. `platform_list_directory`
now fails when the system fails part of the way, as the walk of
`files.c` did on its own.

## Tests first

`platform_layer` now reads `src/anti/` as well. Before the change it
listed the 11 lines (`s28-red-layer.log`). On the Windows VM before the
change (`s28-vm-red-test.log`), `utf8_names` of `test_files.c` failed 4
checks: the directory `unit-files-ü中` was missing under its UTF-16 name,
and the write, the copy and the walk under it failed. The new step of
`unicode_paths` stopped at `anti: cannot read .../pⁿ?/return42.anti`.
The Mac passed both. After the change all three pass on the VM
(`s28-vm-green-test.log`, `s28-vm-green-layer.log`,
`s28-vm-green-unicode.log`), with `unit`, `files_oom` and every `anti_*`
test, 28 in all. `anti_layer` of `test_files.c` checks each new call of
the layer, links included on POSIX.

The changed files compile without a warning under the warning set for
both glibc targets and both Windows targets (`s28-cross.log`). Against
musl every file of `src/anti/` compiles. `src/antic/platform.c` stops at
`siginfo_t` in `sys/wait.h` under the flags of that check, and the file
at `f1ef69dd` stops at the same place (`s28-cross-old.log`).
