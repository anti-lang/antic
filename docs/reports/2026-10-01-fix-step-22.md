# Fix step 22, antic ARM64

Step 22 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M15 and M50. The fix
changed `src/antic/arm64.c` alone, with two new programs, the manifest of
`emit_identity` and `docs/notes/arm64.md`. Logs are in
`build/drive/logs/s22-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M15 | Fixed. `append_add_offset` owns `dst = base + offset`. An offset that no 12-bit immediate holds, shifted or not, goes into `dst` first. `incoming_address`, `copy_argument` and `expand` call it, so the copy in `expand` is gone. The loads and stores of the argument area had the same defect above 32760: `stack_param`, the stores of stack arguments in `emit_call` and the store of an indirect address in `copy_argument`. `based_memory` gives each one a register address when the offset does not fit. |
| M50 | Fixed. `memory()` and `fits_address` take the size of an access from `bits()`, which gives an `f32` 32 bits. `fits_offset` is the one rule of a load or store offset, and the spills use it in place of `fits_scaled`. |

## Tests first

- M15: `programs/stack_argument_offsets.anti`. Before the fix, a `Pair`
  after 519, 520 and 521 `int` arguments printed 41, 2 and 2 where 42 is
  right. The functions with 4104 `int` arguments, one followed by an `int`
  and one by a `Triple` of 24 bytes passed by address, did not assemble:
  llvm-mc refused `ldr x9, [x29, #32776]` and `str x9, [sp, #32768]`.
- M50: `programs/f32_far_field.anti` reads and writes an `f32` at 20000
  and at 16380. Before the fix llvm-mc refused `ldr s0, [x0, #20000]` and
  `str s0, [x0, #20000]`. Both programs pass after the fix
  (`s22-two.log`).

## Pinned outputs

`tests/emit-identity/programs.sha256` gained the twelve lines of the two
programs. The three ARM64 lines of `generic_constants` changed: an `f32`
load with an index now folds as `ldr s19, [x11, x10, lsl #2]` in place of
a separate `lsl` and `ldr`. The scale of 8 kept the shift of 2 from
matching before.
