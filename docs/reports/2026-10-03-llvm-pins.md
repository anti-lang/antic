# Step pins of the LLVM back end

Step `pins` of `docs/work-order-llvm-back-end.md`, the section "Toolchain and packaging".

## What the step built

- `tools/llvm-pin` and `tools/clang-pin` name `23.1.1-anti.5`. `SHA256SUMS` and
  `SHA256SUMS.sig` of the release were downloaded first. The signature verifies against
  `tools/keys/release.pem` with `openssl pkeyutl -verify`, as `tools/fetch-release.cmake`
  does. Each of the 12 lines of `SHA256SUMS` equals its row in the tables of the work
  order, and each digest line of the two pins equals both.
- `tools/check-llvm.cmake` checks the version of `opt` and `llc` and copies both into
  `bin/` of the runtime archive. `tools/get-llvm.cmake` names the seven tools.
- `tools/pinned-tools.cmake` names the seven tools once, in `ANTIC_LLVM_TOOLS`, and
  `antic_llvm_tool_problems` checks that each stands in the pinned directory and reports
  the version of `tools/llvm-version`. `tests/run_pinned_tools.cmake` calls it, so
  `pinned_tools` prints `7 LLVM tools of 23.1.1` and fails on a tool that is missing. A
  directory without `opt` gave `.../bin/opt is missing`.
- `libunwind.a` stands in the clang archive of anti.5, in
  `lib/clang/23/lib/<arch>-unknown-linux-gnu/` beside `libclang_rt.asan.a`, and not in
  the tools archive. `CMakeLists.txt` copies it into `lib/linux-x86_64-glibc/` and
  `lib/linux-arm64-glibc/` of the runtime archive with the ASan runtime.
  `MEMCHECK_LINUX_UNWIND` in `src/antic/linker.h` names it. The Linux link of
  `--memory-checks` takes it after the runtime of AddressSanitizer and before
  `-lpthread`, through `linux_memcheck_libraries`. That function serves the lld link of
  the glibc mode and the link of `--linker platform` alike.
- `tools/pack-anti.cmake` lists no tool by name. Its header said "five LLVM tools" and
  says seven now. The installers unpack the whole tools archive and name no tool, so
  they are unchanged.
- `docs/decisions.md` line 21 names the seven tools, `libunwind.a` and the pin. The
  `libunwind` entry beside `--memory-checks` lost its open wording.
  `docs/distribution.md` under "The LLVM tools" names the seven tools and
  `libunwind.a`, and records the growth of the archives of anti.4 and anti.5 from the
  reports of `anti-lang/llvm-tools`. `docs/notes/hosts-and-harness.md` and
  `docs/toolchain-later.md` name the new frozen tag.

`build/deps/llvm` and `build/deps/clang` were removed, and `cmake --preset host`
downloaded both anti.5 archives and verified them against the pin, `SHA256SUMS` and
the signature (`build/drive/logs/llvm-pins-configure-host.log`). The configure on
anti-linux did the same into its own directories
(`build/drive/logs/llvm-pins-linux-host-build.log`).
