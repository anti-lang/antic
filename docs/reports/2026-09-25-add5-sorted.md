# The sorted collections of round five: BLOCKED at `union`

The step builds `SortedMap<K, V>` and `SortedSet<T>` in
`anti.collection.sorted` as B-trees. Both are built with every operation of
"Maps" and "Sets" in `docs/anti-language-additions.md` apart from `union(o)` of
the set, which needs a change to the parser outside the fence of the step.

## What was done

- `4b01ae1` built both collections, over one base `BTree<T>`, with the tests
  `std_sorted_map`, `std_sorted_set`, `std_sorted_order` and `std_sorted_trap`
  in both modes and the check `sorted_changed`. `sorted_order` walks the tree
  itself after every hundred of three thousand changes: node sizes, equal leaf
  depth, rising order, and the walks forward and backward.
- `abfe380` ported the module to main, where `anti_rt_element_copy` and
  `anti_rt_element_destroy` are gone. A key or a value is copied with `dup`,
  and the base tears down and copies a whole entry with `destroy` and `dup`.
  The walk of a map and of its `range` is now `SortedEntries<K, V>`, whose
  `value` gives `(K, lent *V)`, so `for (k, v) in &m` changes the values.
- `3a0e4d9` writes a map with `str` keys as a JSON object and any other map
  as an array of pairs. `collection/sorted` left the module list of
  `CMakeLists.txt`, since main finds every collection module itself.
- `docs/decisions-sorted.md` holds the decisions, each `[provisional]` one
  with its reason.

## What failed and how it was fixed

- After the rebase onto main both map tests failed to link on
  `_anti_rt_element_copy`. The module now uses `dup` and `destroy`.
- The map's own `destruct` zeroed its entries and the base then ran `destroy`
  on a zeroed `Tag`, which ended with `table not set`. The map's `destruct`,
  `clear` and `copy_place` are gone, and the base's cover the map.
- The copy walk of a map of `Tag` values tears down nothing. The copy form
  of a lent part does not tear down what it reads. The expected counts
  of `owned_walks` say so.
- Importing `anti.reflect` left the dev tests without its symbols. The
  fixture `dev_object_reflect` and the check `sorted_changed` link an object
  of it.
- `std_collection_modules` refused `collection/sorted` in the list of
  `CMakeLists.txt`, which went back to main's text.

## Provisional decisions

Every `[provisional]` entry of `docs/decisions-sorted.md`: the node of eleven
elements, the one block of nodes by index, the place as node times eleven plus
slot, an empty `range` when `lo` is not below `hi`, the `?(K, V)` results, the
walk `(K, lent *V)` of a map, the copy walk alone of a set, the fatal error on
a lent element moved out of order, the copy of a key a call passes, the
allocator of `intersect` and `minus`, and the JSON object of `str` keys through
`anti.reflect.TypeId.Str`.
