## Found outside the fence

`check_unary` of `src/antic/sema.c:1369` checks the operand of a unary `-`
or `~` twice, once for the operator lookup and once in the `switch`. A
chain of prefix minus signs therefore takes time 2^n in the front end. 26
signs take one second, and 40 did not finish in 20 seconds. The depth limit
keeps it on the stack but not fast. The same holds for the build on
`main`. It belongs with step 3, which owns `sema.c`.
