# Fix step 15, anti bind readers

Step 15 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: S39, S43, S44, S46 and
S41 for `bindclang.c`, in `bindapi.c`, `bindclang.c`, `bindexpr.c`,
`bindtype.c` and `bindwrite.c`.

## Findings

| ID | Result | Where |
|---|---|---|
| S39 | fixed | `read_structs` refuses a description that names one struct twice. |
| S43 | fixed | The argument vectors of clang are an `arg_list` of `const char *`, and `process_capture` reads them without a cast. |
| S44 | fixed | `enum_named` skips an enum without a name. |
| S46 | fixed | The evaluator fails past 128 levels and the type parser past 256, each counted across the nested evaluations and parses a lookup or a typedef starts. `group_end` stops at the bound. The probe walk enters no record it stands in, goes 64 records deep at most and enters a record that held no value once. A macro chain and a typedef chain of an AST are bounded by the same counts. |
| S41 | fixed for `bindclang.c` | The clang version and the `#pragma pack` value are read with `strtol`, checking `errno` and the range. |

A value that fails in the evaluator now keeps a type. Before, `-NAME` with
an unknown name passed NULL to `strcmp` in `narrow`.

## Tests

- `unit`, `tests/unit/test_bind.c`, `deep_input`: six expression forms
  and four type forms a million deep fail, and thirty-two deep read.
  `-UNKNOWN` and `~(int)UNKNOWN` fail. Before the fix the unit binary
  crashed with a segmentation fault (`build/drive/logs/s15-unit1-run.log`,
  exit 139).
- `anti_bind_api_malformed`, new: a truncated description, one nested
  100000 deep, a repeated struct, a struct that holds itself under
  `--probe`, a define of a million `-` and one of a million `(`, a field
  type of a million `*`, an alias of itself and sections of the wrong
  kind. Before the fix the repeated struct crashed
  (`build/drive/logs/s15-new1.log`). The struct that holds itself crashed
  alone as well, exit 139, and passes after
  (`s15-red-self.log`, `s15-green-self.log`).
- `anti_bind_clang_malformed`, new, through the pinned clang. It binds an
  unnamed enum before a named one, a macro of 100000 `-` and one of
  100000 parentheses. It adds a chain of 5000 macros and a pack value out
  of range.
  Before the fix it crashed (`build/drive/logs/s15-new2.log`).
- `anti_bind_ast_malformed`, new, not on Windows. A stand-in clang writes
  a version out of range, a truncated AST and one nested 100000 deep. It
  then writes nodes of the wrong kinds and a flat chain of 5000 typedefs. It passed before the
  last two fixes too. The typedef chain was already bounded by the first
  commit. On macOS `atoi` of an out-of-range value returns -1, which is
  not observable. S41 and S43 are undefined behaviour that no build here
  shows, so neither had a failing test.

## Decisions

Every decision is `[provisional]` in `docs/decisions-tool.md`, under
"Step 15, anti bind readers". They are the refusal of a repeated struct,
the two depth bounds and how they count, and the global counters. The
bound of the probe walk and the reading of an out-of-range version and
pack value follow.
