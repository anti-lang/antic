# The safety check index-out-of-range

The step built C2 of "Compile-time checks of 2026-10-20" in
`docs/decisions.md`. Logs are under `build/drive/logs/checks-c2/`.

## What changed

Commit `8aa0d9b8` builds the check. `src/antic/sema_index.c` reads every
index of an array, a slice or a `str` of a function body after C1. It
knows the value of an integer expression as a range whose two ends some
run takes. A constant, `.len` of a fixed array and a local that the
function never assigns or takes the address of after its `let` have one
value. A slice or a `str` that the function never changes has one length,
a number or an unknown of 0 or more. The variable of a `for` over a range
takes every value of its turns, with the step. `+`, `-` and `*` by a
constant follow the values. An index of one value below zero or at the
length is refused wherever it stands, as decision 24 refuses a constant
index. An index of a loop variable is refused when every turn runs to the
end of its body and reaches the index. Every other index keeps the bounds
check of dev mode.

The tests came first and failed, `red.log`. Seven refusals stand in
`tests/matrix/values_index_out_of_range_*`: the off by one of
`0..xs.len + 1`, a step of 2 over an odd length, a constant index on a
slice of known length, an index below zero on a slice, the off by one on a
slice parameter, a local of one value and a write in a loop by -1. One
program of the indices the check allows stands beside them: a `break`, an
`if`, a `continue`, a `return`, a changed local and slice, an address
taken, a `while`, a call, a step inside the range, a range without a turn
and a correct loop over a parameter. `tests/errors/index_out_of_range`
covers a dev build and `unchecked`. `check_bounds_unproved` compiles a
loop that may leave before its last turn, which a dev build stops with
`index out of bounds`.

The guide, the overview and the note of warnings describe the check.

## Mistakes of the task

- C2 names the range `a..=b` and the example `for i in 0..=n { a[i] }`.
  Anti has no `..=`, and the feature freeze adds no form. The check reads
  it as `a..b + 1`, by a provisional entry, and the tests use
  `0..xs.len + 1`. The column of the fix in the note of warnings reads `0..n` in
  place of `0..n + 1`.
- "Bounds checks" of chapter 12 of the guide said that `a[4]` of a
  `[4]int` compiles and is checked by no mode. Decision 24 refuses it, and
  lowering checks a constant index as any other. The guide now says so.

## Provisional entries added

Four, after the entries of C1 in `docs/decisions.md`. `a..=b` read as
`a..b + 1`. What a local of one value, an unknown parameter and the
length of a slice are, and the bound of 2^40 on a followed value. When an
index of one value and an index of a loop variable are refused, and that a
call counts as returning. The check covers an index of an array, a slice
and a `str`, and no pointer, `index` hook or part `a[lo..hi]`.
