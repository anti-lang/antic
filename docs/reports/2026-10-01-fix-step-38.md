# Fix step 38, the standard collections

Step 38 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` closes M27, M08, M28
and M01 in `anti.collection` and its modules. Logs are in
`build/drive/logs/s38-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M28 | Fixed. `ConcurrentMap` hashed each entry as `(k.hash(), v.hash()).hash()`, which mixes the key once more than the default hash of the tuple. `collection_hash` now compares a `ConcurrentMap` with a `HashMap` of the same entries, and printed `hash alike 0` before the fix (`s38-m28-red.log`). |
| M08 | Fixed. `map.anti` copied `struct anti_field` as `Record` and `ANTI_TYPE_STR` as `TYPE_STR`, and `sorted.anti` imported `anti.reflect` for `TypeId.Str`. The dev tests of the sorted collections and of `dup`, `hash` and `equals` now link without the object of `anti.reflect`, and failed with undefined `anti.reflect` symbols before (`s38-m08-red.log`). |
| M27 | Fixed. `Copies.new` and `PriorityQueue.new` went on empty when the allocator had run out. `queue_no_room` and `copies_no_room` printed "not reached" before the fix and now stop in `new` with exit 1 (`s38-m27-red.log`). |
| M01 | Fixed, by Eddie's decision. `errors/collection_objects.anti` compiled before the removal (`s38-m01-red.log`) and is now refused for each of the three names. |

## Fixes

- M28: `collection.hash_sum` sums the hashes of the elements and
  `collection.hash_of_sum` mixes the sum with the count.
  `hash_in_any_order` calls both, and `ConcurrentMap.hash_entries` adds
  `hash_sum` over its parts and mixes the total once.
- M08: the runtime owns the record, so `anti_rt_arg_is_str` of
  `src/rt/object.c` says whether a type argument is `str`.
  `collection.text_keys` asks it. `open_entries`, `write_entry`,
  `write_entry_texts`, `write_entry_text` and `close_entries` of
  `anti.collection` write the entries of `Map`, `HashMap` and
  `SortedMap`. `Record`, `kind_of`, the type numbers, `bracket` and the
  import of `anti.reflect` are gone. The runtime change moved the digest
  of `return42`, and `link_identity_macos-arm64` holds the one this Mac
  gives.
- M27: `anti.collection.Indexed<T>` is the base of `List`, `Deque`,
  `Ring`, `Grid`, `PriorityQueue` and `Copies`. It holds the room, the
  five place functions with `slot(i)` as the one map from position to
  slot, `make_room` and `resize`, the walk `IndexedWalk<T>`,
  `copy_into` for `dup` and the teardown. `Deque` and `Ring` set
  `ring`, and `Grid` and `PriorityQueue` keep one spare slot. The five
  walk classes are gone. `moved` and `close_gap` stay on `Collection<T>`,
  since a collection of a program uses them, as `collection_parts`
  shows. `list.anti`, `deque.anti`, `ring.anti`, `grid.anti` and
  `queue.anti` lose 563 lines, and `Indexed<T>` with its walk takes 230
  in `collection.anti`.
- M01: `List`, `Map`, `IntMap`, `Entry`, `hash_text` and `hash_int` are
  gone. `tests/std/collection.anti`, their one user, holds its objects in
  `List<*Item>`, `Map<str, *Item>` and `HashMap<int, *Item>`.

## Decisions

Two `[provisional]` entries in `docs/decisions.md`, under "Generics and
collections":

- `anti.collection.Indexed<T>` is the public abstract base of the six
  collections indexed from 0, with the parts it holds.
- `IndexedWalk<T>` is the public iterator of `List`, `Deque`, `Ring`,
  `Grid` and `Copies`, in place of `ListWalk` and its four siblings.

Four entries change in place. They cover the hash of `ConcurrentMap`
(M28) and the reading of the key type of a map (M08). They also cover
`new` of every collection over `Indexed<T>`, which stops when the
allocator has run out (M27), and the removal of the collections over
objects (M01). The overview drops the sentence on those.
