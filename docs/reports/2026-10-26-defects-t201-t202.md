# The defects T201 and T202

The step closes the last two findings of `docs/audit/tests-findings.md`
that the Mac shows. Logs stand in `build/drive/logs/defects/`.

## Fixes

| Finding | Commit | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| T201 | `8d3f0922` | Each symbol records the `defer` and `undo` blocks around its declaration. A name marks a local as named by a deferred block only inside a block that the local stands outside, so a local declared in a `defer` moves into an `own` parameter there. |
| T202 | `96a09525` | A generic body folds its constants with its type parameters open, and `carried_value` writes them into the library file. A copy made in another module computes each with its arguments, so `lib.Gen<u8>.SIZE` gives 1. |

T202 follows Eddie's decision of 2026-10-26, recorded after the gaps of
the step `points` under "Answers of 2026-10-24" in `docs/decisions.md`.
The DESIGN comment of `carried_value` says why, and the format version
is 93: `antl.h`, the first bytes of `scale.antl.hex` and
`generics/pick.antl.hex`, and the two checks of the version in
`tests/unit/test_modules.c`.

The expected output of `modules_generic_constant` began with `exit 0`.
The runner of `tests/modules/` compares the output alone, so the line
was a mistake of the test, which its listing as a known failure hid.
