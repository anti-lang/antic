"Decisions of 2026-10-11": `--llvm-mc` goes, and llvm-mc leaves the
package. The logs are in `build/drive/logs/s27/`.

The `if(ANTIC_LLVM_MC)` guards of `tests/CMakeLists.txt` could never be
false and are gone. `run_program.cmake` no longer stops on a missing
llvm-mc.

The tests of `antic -S`, of the Windows unwind data and of the
assembly excerpts of the developer's guide still assemble with llvm-mc.
They take `ANTIC_LLVM_MC` of `build/deps`, which the build installs and no
package carries. `llvm_llc_failed`, which step 26 left to this step, keeps
llvm-mc of the build as the tool that refuses the options of llc.

The documents follow: the entries of `docs/decisions.md` on lld, the LLVM
tools, the tools of a build, the package and the driver options,
`docs/distribution.md`, `docs/tooling.md`, `docs/tooling-addendum.md`,
`docs/libraries-for-c.md`, `docs/notes/llvm.md`, "Debug information" of
`docs/anti-language-additions.md`, which the overview already words
the same, two pages of the developer's guide, the page of the runtime
archive, `docs/vm-setup.md` and the toolchain rule of `CLAUDE.md`.
