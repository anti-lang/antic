The `if(ANTIC_LLVM_MC)` guards of `tests/CMakeLists.txt` could never be
false and are gone. `run_program.cmake` no longer stops on a missing
llvm-mc.

assembly excerpts of the developer's guide still assemble with llvm-mc.

package carries. `llvm_llc_failed`, which step 26 left to this step, keeps
llvm-mc of the build as the tool that refuses the options of llc.
