# Pin 23.1.1-anti.7

Item 1 of the session that closes the move to LLVM. `86dd1264` moves `tools/llvm-pin`
and `tools/clang-pin` to `23.1.1-anti.7` of `anti-lang/llvm-tools`, the release whose
static musl tools ask for a thread stack of 8 MiB.

## The digests

- `gh release view 23.1.1-anti.7 -R anti-lang/llvm-tools` shows the release published
  on 2026-10-07 with its twelve archives.
- `openssl dgst -sha256 -verify tools/keys/release.pem -signature SHA256SUMS.sig
  SHA256SUMS` printed `Verified OK`.
- The twelve lines of `../llvm-tools/docs/reports/2026-10-07-llvm-tools-thread-stack.md`
  equal the twelve lines of `SHA256SUMS`, sorted, with `diff` silent.
- `build/deps/llvm` and `build/deps/clang` were removed, and `cmake --preset host`
  downloaded both archives of macos-arm64 and checked each against the pin, the line of
  `SHA256SUMS` and the signature.
- Both VMs took the anti.7 archives of their host from the same pins with
  `tools/get-clang.cmake` and `tools/get-llvm.cmake`. The anti.6 copies stand beside
  them as `clang-anti6` and `toolchain-anti6`. `llvm-readobj --program-headers` of the
  `ld.lld` of anti-linux reads a `PT_GNU_STACK` of 8,388,608 bytes.

## The ThinLTO link

On anti-linux, `antic --lto thin` linked `tests/bench/ablate/mixed_work.anti` for
linux-arm64 at `armv8.0` three times of three, and each program printed
`615670597359`. The crash of anti.6 needed the runtime bitcode with the musl triple of
before `45e8bcee`, which the report of anti.7 reproduced. This run used the bitcode of
today.

## Documents

`docs/decisions.md`, `docs/distribution.md`, `docs/notes/hosts-and-harness.md` and
`docs/toolchain-later.md` name anti.7. `docs/distribution.md` states what anti.7
changed: the header field of the 18 static Linux binaries and `VERSION`.
