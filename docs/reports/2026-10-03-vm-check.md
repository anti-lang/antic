decisions recorded at the end of that report. Then it runs every suite
again on the Mac, anti-linux and anti-windows with the pinned toolchain. Both VMs ran
the export of `a4cadfcf`, which is `origin/main`.

## What changed, and what failed

- The stack of the compiler (`cfed8d9f`). `platform_run_on_stack` of
  `src/antic/platform.c` runs work on a thread of `PLATFORM_WORK_STACK`,
  64 MB, through `pthread_attr_setstacksize` or `CreateThread`. antic, anti
  and the unit runner run on it. `antic_stack` compiles the deepest source
  the parser accepts under `ulimit -s 256`. It ended by SIGSEGV before
  (`vmc2-mac-red-stack.log`). `deep_stack` of `unit_tool_platform` takes
  32 MB on the helper. `unit_modules` of the Mac's UBSan tree overflowed
  under an 8 MB stack before and passes after
  (`vmc2-mac-ubsan-modules-4096.log`, `-green-4096.log`). On anti-linux it
  now passes under UBSan.
- The unwinder (`7bc666f1`). `MEMCHECK_OPTIONS` holds
  `fast_unwind_on_fatal=1`. `memory_checks` decodes the options from the
  assembly of each target, and failed on the old string
  (`vmc2-mac-mc-red2.log`). The pin of libunwind is the open item: the
  newest release of `anti-lang/llvm-tools` is `23.1.1-anti.4`.
- `linux_libc` failed once on anti-linux (`vmc2-linux-host-suite.log`) and
  passed alone. Every configure runs `tools/get-llvm.cmake`, which copied
  lld over `ld.lld`, `ld64.lld` and `lld-link` each time. `deps_dir`
  configures a copy against the same directory while the suite runs
  `ld.lld` from it. The copy is made only when a name holds something else
  (`a4cadfcf`). `llvm_names` runs the script on a stand-in tree and failed
  before the fix (`vmc2-mac-llvm-names-red.log`).

## Decisions

- Eddie's two decisions stand in `docs/decisions.md`: the stack under
  "Compiler behaviour", and the unwinder beside the entries on
  `--memory-checks`.
- `[provisional]` added: anti runs its work on the same helper, and the unit
  runner runs every group on it. anti runs the compiler in its own process.

## Open

- The pin of `23.1.1-anti.5` with libunwind, the runtime archive that
  carries it and the Linux link of `--memory-checks` that takes it. The
  release does not exist yet.
