  `tests/package_tree.cmake`, each in a directory of its own. The

## A defect seen on Windows, without a row

At `1ba2cce5` `antic_option_linker_lld` failed on anti-windows in the
whole run and in the run after it, and then passed three times. A tree
of `c4fe4db4` beside it passed it four times, `s26-win-lld2.log`,
`s26-win-base.log` and `s26-win-cmp.log`. The step changes neither the
test nor what it builds. It compares the bytes of two links of
`return42.anti`. A Windows executable holds the time of its link at
offset 0x80 and in its debug directory, since antic passes `/Brepro` to
the link of a library alone, by R152. Two links of one program two
seconds apart differed in those two bytes alone, `s26-ts.cmd` of
`build/s26/`. So the test fails whenever its two links fall in two
seconds.

It has no row in `docs/audit/tests-findings.md`. An open row needs a line
of `tests/known-failures.txt`, and a listed test that passes fails the
suite. A test that fails in some runs can stand in neither state.
Whether a Windows executable must be reproducible is a choice of the
build that no document makes, and it is Eddie's. The two ways are
`/Brepro` for every link and a test that compares the program without
the time.
