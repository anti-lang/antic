# Solid step 14: anti bind

Step 14 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`. The step started at
`d90f3efa`. Logs are under `build/drive/logs/`, named `step14-*`.

## Findings closed

- S319, `e1a931d8`. `string` of `bindexpr.c` moved two bytes past a
  backslash before it looked at the byte after it, so a text that ended
  in the backslash sent `space` past the terminator. `unit_bind` feeds
  such a text in an allocation of its exact size, which AddressSanitizer
  refused before the fix, `step14-s319-red.log`.
- M311, `2937296e`. `apply` tested the double for a finite value before
  it narrowed to a float, and `converted` narrowed no double at all, so
  `3e38f * 10` and `(float)1e300` became `inf.0`. One function narrows
  every float and fails a value past `FLT_MAX`.
- M312, `678ec24a`. Both readers parsed the spelling of a typedef again
  at every use. The reader of a header keeps the type of a typedef while
  nothing it may name changes, and the reader of a description keeps the
  type of an alias or a callback. `api_typedef_chain` and
  `clang_typedef_chain` ran past their 30 seconds before,
  `step14-m312-red.log`. The second stands a clang of the shell in: a
  real clang nests the chain in its dump of every typedef, 6.4 GB for a
  chain of twelve.
- S333, `d62c120a`. `bind_is_keyword` answered true for every text that
  is no identifier, and the reader of a header held a name to no rule.
  Both readers now take the test of a C identifier and its message from
  `bindmodel.c`. The test of a word of Anti is `bind_is_anti_word`. Ten
  cases `anti_bind_message_clang_name_*` hold the refusals.
- The minor finding on names, `bindwrite.c:263`, `8b920c34`. A record,
  an enum, an enumerator, a constant and the names the probe of Anti
  reaches were written as C spells them. They take the trailing `_` of a
  field. A define of such a name is skipped, as a macro is. `words` and
  `api_words` compile the module and compare the probes.
- M339, the first two pairs. `f5887976`: `settable` had its own test of
  an unsigned type and took a bitfield of one bit of `size_t` for a
  signed one. `7be83886`: a `COLOR` define and a compound literal are read
  by `bind_struct_const`, so a define of floats or of an expression binds
  as the macro does. The last two pairs belong to step 21.
- T140, `89b9938a`. `find_clang` cut the directory of anti at `/` alone.
  Step 13 left it to this step.
- T137, `54956c22`. The reader passed over the fixed base of an enum of
  C23. Its cause holds on every host: `enum Small : unsigned char` took
  `c_int`, 4 bytes where C lays out 1. `fixed_enum` shows it on the Mac,
  `step14-t137-red.log`. Step 1 left it to the step of the commands of
  anti, and `bindclang.c` is a file of this step.

`ed695362` puts the rule of names into the chapter on C of the guide.
`92f96ecb` records the commits in the summary and in
`docs/audit/tests-findings.md`.
