# Fix step 5: antic checker context

Step 5 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: S04, from
`docs/audit/front-end.md`. The logs lie in `build/drive/logs/s5-*.log`.

## Findings

| Id | Result |
|---|---|
| S04 | Fixed, `cb4794d` |

`struct context` in `sema_checker.h` holds the scope, the function,
`within`, the signature, the enclosing `try`, `quiet` and
`target_sized`. `sema_enter` and `sema_leave` in `sema.c` are its only
writers. `sema_enter_scope` keeps the outer context in the scope and
`sema_leave_scope` puts it back. Every hand-written save the finding
lists is gone: the passes of `sema.c` restore `within` instead of
setting it to NULL, and `sema_const_symbol`, `const_deps_of_symbol`,
`sema_alias_type` and `resolve_params` no longer save a subset.

Each part of the finding has a test that failed before the fix
(`s5-unfixed2.log`) and passes after it:

- The constant in a signature. `programs/checker_context.anti` is the
  finding's example. Before the fix antic refused `sized<9>(a)` with
  "expected `[9 + 1]byte`". A constant of the module or of a class is
  now evaluated in the context of its declaration, which
  `sema_declaration_context` gives. Aliases and constraint sets take the
  same context.
- The quiet probe. `errors/quiet_constant.anti` holds a class constant
  `1 / 0`. `refuse_nested_in_signatures` evaluates it first, quietly.
  Before the fix antic crashed when a body named the constant, and
  accepted the program when none did. The declaration context is never
  quiet, so the probe caches no result a loud check would not. The
  constant reports its error at its own place. The refusal of a chain of constants that is too deep is
  reported in the same context. A local constant needs neither: each
  check of its statement declares a new symbol.
- The `try`. `programs/nested_try.anti` holds a `try` ending in
  `catch fatal` inside another `try`. Before the fix the outer
  `catch e` bound an error type and antic crashed. Each `try` block
  now has a `struct try_record` of its own, which the context points at
  and the failing calls of its body fill, so leaving the inner block
  gives the outer record back untouched. An anonymous function and a
  function body start with no `try` around them.

The emit manifest gained the lines of the two new programs alone.
Every other program emits the same assembly on all six targets
(`s5-ei-write2.log`).

The fix touched `sema_call.c`, `sema_copies.c`, `sema_expr.c` and
`sema_safety.c` beside the files the step names, since they wrote or
read the fields that moved.

Seen in passing and left, since it lies outside the step: `anti fmt`
indents the statement after `} catch fatal` as a continuation line.
