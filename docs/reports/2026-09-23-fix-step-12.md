# Fix step 12: runtime shared state, blocked

Step 12 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` names S33, S34, S35,
M19, M22 and M30 in `src/rt/conf.c`, `loaded.c`, `plugin.c`, `signal.c`,
`threads.c` and `atomic.c`. Two of the findings need a file outside that
list, so the step stopped before any code changed. No log was written.

## Findings

- S33, left. `set_key` in `conf.c` writes `anti_rt_option_backtrace`,
  and `anti_rt_backtrace_on` in `src/rt/backtrace.c:22` reads it on every
  `fail`, from any thread. An atomic store in `conf.c` alone leaves a
  plain read on the other side, which is still a data race under rule 23.
  The fix needs an atomic load in `src/rt/backtrace.c`, which the step
  does not name.
- S34, left. `anti_rt_registry_find` in `src/rt/registry.c:72` walks
  `classes` of each open library through `anti_rt_plugin_registry`, and
  reads the pointer it gets after the call returns. A lock inside
  `loaded.c` cannot cover that walk, and an unload may unmap the image
  while it runs. The fix the audit gives, one lock around load, unload
  and the walks, needs the walk in `src/rt/registry.c` to take the lock.
  Step 13 names `registry.c`, and step 12 does not.
- S35, M19, M22 and M30, left. Each lies inside the fence and needs no
  other file, but the step stops as a whole on a change outside it.
