# Fix step 25, antic allocator

Step 25 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M03 and the minor
out-of-memory exits. The fix added `src/antic/alloc.c` and
`src/antic/alloc.h`, emptied the allocator out of `ir.c` and `ir.h`, and
changed every caller in `src/antic/`. `tools/sources.cmake` lists the new
file. Logs are in `build/drive/logs/s25-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M03 | Fixed. `alloc.c` owns the heap memory of antic: `alloc_zeroed`, `alloc_resize`, `alloc_grow`, `alloc_product`, `alloc_sum` and `alloc_out_of_memory`. `header.c` no longer includes `ir.h`. `ir_vformat` and `ir_format`, which the back-end report names as the same kind of helper, are `text_vformat` and `text_format` of `text.c`. |
| Out-of-memory exits, minor (structure, back-end) | Fixed. The 49 hand-written blocks in 23 files are calls of the allocator, among them the local `grow` of `sema_chain.c` and `sema_copies.c`, `out_of_memory` of `antl_tree.c` and `zeroed` of `ptrset.c`. The pattern literals of `lower_pattern.c` exited with 1 and now exit with 70. `expand.c` and `coff.c` call the allocator. `process.c` and `selfpath.c` returned a failure on a refused allocation and now end the run like every other part. `widen` of `process.c` still returns NULL for text that is not UTF-8, and its message now says so instead of "out of memory". `join_coff` of `driver.c` no longer fails without a message on an empty list. |
| Unchecked sums of `arena.c` and `text.c`, minor | Fixed with the allocator, since both are its callers. `align_up`, the block size of `arena_alloc` and `reserve` of `text.c` use `alloc_sum` and `alloc_product`. Step 40 lists this row, and it has nothing left to do there. |

The blocks that grew from a first capacity of their own, 4, 16, 64 or
256, now start at the 8 of `alloc_grow`. No caller reads the capacity
for anything but growth.

## Tests first

`one_allocator`, a new test, reads every file of `src/antic/`. It
refuses a call of `malloc`, `calloc` or `realloc`, the out-of-memory
message, `exit(70)` and `exit(1)` outside `alloc.c` and `alloc.h`. It
also refuses an IR header in the lexer, parser, checker, types, C header
and tree dumps. Before the fix it failed on `alloc.c` missing and on
every copy (`s25-red-test.log`).

`alloc_oom` runs `tests/unit/test_alloc_oom.c`, which asks each helper,
the memory pool and the text buffer for more memory than any host gives
and expects status 70 with the message. Before the fix it did not
compile (`s25-red-build.log`). Against the old `arena.c` and `text.c`,
`arena_alloc(SIZE_MAX - 4)` handed back memory for a size that wrapped
(`s25-red2-arena.log`), and the text buffer died with SIGSEGV
(`s25-red2-text.log`).

The Windows branches of `selfpath.c` and `process.c`, which no build on
the Mac compiles, were checked with the pinned clang for
`x86_64-pc-windows-msvc` against the sysroot, with no warning
(`s25-win-*.log`).

## Decisions

Two `[provisional]` entries in `docs/decisions.md` under the gaps of
the first audit were rewritten, not added:

- the checked allocator is `alloc.c`, the one file that calls the C
  allocator, held by `one_allocator`. The entry named `ir.c` and said
  the front end kept its own copies until a later step moved them.
- the three dots of a cut message are written by `text_vformat`.

No new `[provisional]` entry.
