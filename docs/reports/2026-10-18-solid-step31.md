# Step 31, the "Files of" sections and rule 10

Step 31 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` closes M352 and M364.
The logs stand in `build/drive/logs/` under the prefix `s31-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| M352 | `6d0fc3f6` | The checker grew from six files to eighteen and parts moved between them, and the entries of `docs/decisions.md` were not read against the new files. |
| M364 | `93df3995` | Rule 10 of `docs/c-guidelines.md` named a test of the banned calls that was never written. |

M352. The seven sentences stood as the front-end report gives them. The
entry of the checker names eighteen files and points at the comment of
`sema_checker.h` and at `docs/notes/source-tree.md`, which
`header_sections` and `source_tree` keep whole. The other entries give
the `provides` and `link` checks to `sema.c`, `sync` to `sema_stmt.c`,
the aliases and the strip to `sema_generic.c`, the default `==` to
`sema_hash.c` and the field inits of a literal to `sema_call.c`.
`parser_item.c` reads the fields of a body. The entry of `ast_walk.c`
names the five analyses that use the walk and the five that recurse on
their own. The header of `ast_walk.c` and its row in the note said the
same stale sentence, and the commit corrects both. The other four
"Files of" sections already name every file of their part.

M364. `tests/runners/run_banned_calls.cmake`, the test `banned_calls`,
refuses a call of `strcpy`, `strcat`, `sprintf`, `vsprintf`, `gets` or
`strncpy`, and a call of the scanf family with a bare `%s`, in a file
of `src/rt/`, `src/antic/` or `src/anti/`. It first checks its own
pattern on twelve lines, so a pattern that finds nothing fails it,
`s31-banned-broken.log`. Without its one exception it fails on the
three calls of `src/rt/platform_stdio.c`, `s31-banned-noexcl.log`. That
file defines `sprintf`, `vsprintf` and the scanf family for a Windows
target, so the test leaves it out and rule 10 says so.

`M352` and `M364` are marked fixed in the summary by `1d3c9dfd`.
