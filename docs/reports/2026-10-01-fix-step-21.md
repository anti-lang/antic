# Fix step 21, antic reach and folding

Step 21 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M16 and M18. The fix
needed two new sources of `src/antic/`, `reach.c` with `reach.h` and
`ir_fold.c` with `ir_fold.h`, and changes to `whole.c`, `optimize.c`,
`layout.c`, `lower_expr.c` and `tools/sources.cmake`. Logs are in
`build/drive/logs/s21-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M16 | Fixed. `ir_reach` of `src/antic/reach.c` holds the one rule of what a program reaches, and `remove_unused_functions` of the optimizer and `whole_program` both call it. `reach_program`, `struct reach` and the marking of the optimizer are gone. The rule takes the entries by `ir_in_unit` and follows a live declaration of a datum to its definition, as the optimizer did. A work list replaces the recursion of `mark_function`. |
| M18 | Fixed. `ir_fold_int` of `src/antic/ir_fold.c` folds every integer operation of two operands for both `fold_op` of the layout and `fold_inst` of the optimizer. It returns a status for a division by zero, the least value by -1 and a shift count outside the width. The layout refuses each with a message, and the optimizer leaves it to run. `shift_wrap_sym` clamps the count of a symbolic `<<%` to 0 outside the width, so the mask still gives the 0 of the rule. |

## Tests first

- M16: `reach_follows_declarations` and `reach_takes_the_unit` of
  `tests/unit/test_whole.c`. In the first, `main` names `lib.hook` through
  its own declaration, and only lib's definition names a function that reads
  the registry. In the second, a library holds a copy of a generic made in
  its unit. Before the fix both failed at the check that the registry
  exists, while the optimizer kept the function (`s21-unit1.log`).
- M18: `shift_out_of_range` of `tests/unit/test_layout.c` folds
  `1 << (size_of(c_long) * 8)` and its two right shifts. It wants an error
  on linux-x86_64, and 2^32 on windows-x86_64. The listing
  `errors/layout_shift.anti` compiles the audit's probe for linux-x86_64.
  Before the fix both failed, the probe compiled, and the unit test folded
  `1 << 64` to 1 (`s21-m18a.log`).
- `folds_one_rule` of `tests/unit/test_optimize.c` pins `ir_fold_int`.
  `programs/wrapping.anti` gained `1 <<% (size_of(c_long) * 16)` and
  `1 <<% -size_of(c_long)`. Both print 0, which needs the clamp once the
  fold refuses the count.

## Seen and left

A target-dependent constant in a function body is not refused.
`let k: u64 = (1 as u64) << ((size_of(c_long) * 8) as u64);` compiles to
`shlq $64` for linux-x86_64, and `7 / (size_of(c_long) - 8)` to an `idivq`
by 0. The rule of `docs/decisions.md` makes both a compile error "in a
constant and in a function body". The shift is an instruction whose count
the back end folds after the checker. Refusing it needs a check in the back
end after that fold, outside this step's files. Should the back end refuse
it, or does a dev-mode check at run time suffice for a function body?
