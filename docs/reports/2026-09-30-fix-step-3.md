S03 and S24. The verifier `src/antic/antl_verify.c` holds a struct marked
simd in the type table, `antl_verify_simd_type`, and an aggregate marked
simd in the IR, `antl_verify_simd_agg`, to one shape, `simd_shape`. The
lanes are whole, of one type of one width on every target, a power of
two of them and a multiple of 8 bytes. The struct is neither packed nor
aligned. A mask is the one exception to the size, since `types_mask`
gives it one `bool` lane per lane of the struct it compares. `ir_lane` is
gone. The IR verifier refuses a simd operation on a struct above the
vector cap. Lowering writes each operation there as a loop, so the cap
is a rule of the operation and not of the table, which holds such a
struct legally. `ir_lane_bytes` of `ir.c` gives the width of a lane to
both verifiers.

`damaged_simd` in `tests/unit/test_modules.c` pokes every type of the
table into the lane `zq`, `ZZ` of no bytes among them, in both tables. It
makes structs of three lanes and of four bytes simd, packs and aligns
`ZV`, changes its lane count and cuts the file at every length. It also
points a sum at `ZW`, 512 bytes of lanes. Before the fix the reader
accepted 36 pokes and the verifier the sum (`s3-red-unit.log`).

S23 and the two reader gaps. `antl_verify_const` refuses the type of an
aggregate on every constant but an aggregate, and void on a constant
that holds a value. An empty constant keeps void, as lowering writes it.
The alignment of a global is a power of two or 0, and `none` stands only
for a pointer or a function that may be `none`. `damaged_constants` gives
the first item of a descriptor's value both types, aligns the global to
3 and gives the default `none` of a field every type of the table. All
were accepted before the fix.

Left. The lexer's cap on diagnostics belongs to step 4, which covers the
lexer. `m.<digits>` is a rule of the checker and falls to the minor
findings of the front end, step 40. Neither is a reader of library files.
