## Findings left

- Rule 15: four forms of `copy_failed` have no test. Two are "since the
  call names no copy of it" and "since its class is nested in a generic".
  The other two are "since the copy of its class lacks it" and "since it
  is named as a value and not called". The checker refuses each case earlier. For example,
  `let g = same;` gives "`same` is generic, and names a function where it
  is called". No program reached the other three.
- Repeated blocks: the digit and letter tests of `lexer.c`, `pattern.c` and
  `modpath.c` stay separate. The lexer reads `int` with -1 at the end,
  and the pattern reader reads `unsigned` code points. Under
  `-Wconversion`, a shared test would need a conversion at every call.
- Placement: `sema.h` includes `antic.h` for `struct package`. That moves
  with the back-end finding on the records in `antic.h`. `destroy_of_local`
  checks the `destroy` expression and stays in `sema_expr.c`, beside its
  only caller. The "Files of the checker" entries in `docs/decisions.md`
  keep the checks of `provides`, `link` and the simd struct in `sema.c`.
  They also keep the field inits of a literal in `sema_call.c`. The step
  follows those entries, and the header of `sema.c` now says so.
- Rules 18 and 19 belong to step 34, which names them.
