# Solid: step 6, indices and sizes of the library

The step fixed the findings of step 6 of "Fix steps" in
`docs/audit/summary.md`: S315, S316 and M304. It ran in one session on
2026-10-16. The logs lie in `build/drive/logs/`, under names that start
with `step6-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| S316 | `f393ed52`, with `9057c609` | The size of the room of a collection was a product or a sum that no build checked, so a release build wrapped it and made room smaller than the collection counted. |
| S315 | `b81ca20c` | The place functions of `Collection<T>` are public, as every abstract function is, and each collection indexed its room with the place it was given. |
| M304 | `2004bca3` | `reserve` and `shrink` replaced the room a walk holds and counted no change, so a dev build walked freed room with no trap. |

`0a7446b1` writes the commit of each into its row of the summary, and
`4f787a6e` writes the new sources in the form of `anti fmt`.

- S316. `room_times` and `room_plus`, internal functions of
  `anti.collection`, compute each size with the flags form and stop
  through `anti_rt_out_of_memory` with the largest `int`, by decision 4
  of "Decisions of 2026-10-11". They stand in `room_from`, the cells of
  a `Grid`, the blocks of both map tables, the nodes of the B-tree, the
  words of a `BitSet`, the blocks of a `Pool`, the growth of a
  `Snapshot` and every `reserve`. A `Pool` refuses a capacity above
  2^62, where the shift of `prepare` never ended. The eleven `room_`
  programs of `tests/traps/` failed before the fix, `step6-red-s316.log`,
  and `room_pool_shift` hung. The four read in `step6-red-s316-v.log`
  ran to their end with a wrapped size and stopped nothing. The fix
  first checked the sum of `make_room` as well, which put an overflow
  test into every `push` and broke the excerpt of chapter 16 of the
  guide. `9057c609` keeps the plain sum there with a PERF DECISION, since
  every caller adds one element.
- S315. `next_place`, `lend_place`, `copy_place` and `take_place` of
  every collection check the place and stop through `Error.fatal` when
  it holds no element, as `out_of_order` of `sorted.anti` stops:
  `` `take_place` of `Pool<int>` was given a place that holds no
  element ``. The message names no place, since the place of a hashed
  collection depends on the seed. `first_place` and the walks inside a
  table call the unchecked helpers, `live_after` of `EntryTable` among
  them. The twelve `place_` programs, one per class that fills the
  functions, failed before the fix, `step6-red-s315.log` and
  `step6-red-s315-tree.log`. `place_map_next` stopped with a bus error.
  `Held` of `concurrent.anti`, which no program can name, takes the check
  as well.
- M304. `reserve` and `shrink` take `at: lang.SourceLocation = here`, as
  the functions that change the size do, and count a change when the room
  moves. The twelve checks `check_<collection>_reserve_changed` and
  `_shrink_changed` failed before the fix, each dev program exiting with
  7, `step6-red-m304.log`. The specification, the overview and chapter 16
  of the guide say that a move of the room is a change.

## The freeze

The step adds no public item. `room_times` and `room_plus` are
`internal`. The parameter `at` of `reserve` and `shrink` has a default,
so every call stays valid. M304 cannot be fixed without it: the entry on
`Changes` in `docs/decisions.md` writes the place of every change from
that parameter, and the trap names it.
