## Comments rewritten

- `param_facts` of `src/antic/llvm_emit_call.c`, by item 2. Old: "A scalar is noundef, an own pointer noalias, and a `*T` nonnull and dereferenceable". New: a scalar is noundef and a `*T` nonnull and dereferenceable. An own pointer is not noalias, since a plain pointer may reach the object while the call runs, T366.
- Above `lower_snapshot` of `src/antic/lower_function.c`, by item 4. Old: "At an `own` field or a `keep own` parameter the record goes on the heap". It went on: "Anywhere else it sits in a slot of the frame". New: at an `own` field, a `keep own` parameter and a local that its `let` gives it, the record goes on the heap. At a parameter that does not keep it, the caller waits, so it sits in the frame.
- The comment of `c_name_read` in `src/antic/types.h`, by item 3. Old: "a module that uses the copy keeps the symbols of its generic, and the program holds two copies". New: the module of the `type`, `c_name_home`, defines the symbols. A module that uses the copy refers to them, and the program holds one copy.
- `LAYOUT_MEMBER_LIMIT` of `src/antic/layout.h`, by item 7. Old: the members of an aggregate are recorded up to 32 bytes. Every convention passes a larger aggregate in memory. A float aggregate of AAPCS64 has at most four members of 8 bytes. New: they are recorded up to 64 bytes, since AAPCS64 passes up to four vectors of 16 bytes in vector registers, T375. Every other aggregate above the size a convention takes in registers still passes in memory.
- Two plain comments of item 4, in `src/antic/sema_stmt.c` and `src/antic/sema_pattern.c`, said that a local never owns a function. Both now say that only the `let` of a snapshot gives a local one.
- New DESIGN comments: `offsets_apart` of `src/antic/optimize.c` (T376), and `refuse_borrowed_snapshot` and the owning `let` of `src/antic/sema_stmt.c` (item 4). `enum_check` gains a sentence by item 5.
