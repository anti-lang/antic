# Fix step 1: the tree relations of the library reader

Step 1 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: S01, from `tool-pass`,
`front-end`, `input-readers` and `back-end`, and the part of M43 for the
generic trees. The logs lie in `build/drive/logs/s1-*.log`.

## Findings

| Id | Result |
|---|---|
| S01 | Fixed, `09ad158` |
| M43, generic trees | Fixed, `09ad158` |

S01. `src/antic/antl_verify.c` is the start of one verifier of library
files. `read_tree` calls it once a tree is read, and a tree it refuses is
damaged. It walks from the function of the tree along what the copy pass
and lowering follow, with one mark per record. It holds the tree to the
rules the checker keeps.


- `otherwise_at` is at most the count of arms. The case of an arm, a
  cast and a struct literal lies inside the variant. `enum_value` lies
  inside the enum, and each lane of a shuffle inside its simd struct.
- A name that lowering evaluates has its symbol, and such an expression
  its type. Every kind of node has the operands and bodies it needs. A
  pattern has its element, and a walk at least one name.
- The parameters of each function match its type, with `self` and the
  out pointer of `may fail`. `copy_count` matches the type parameters of
  the generic and its type.
- `break` and `continue` stand inside a loop of their own function.
- The tree is at most `ANTL_TREE_DEPTH_MAX` deep, and no record holds
  itself. No statement, block, written type or anonymous function stands
  in two places.

The reader gives each table one array, so a mark is found by address. It
compares the lane count of a shuffle with its simd struct. The back end's
additions, `l->loop` at a `break` and `tuple->fields[i]` of a pattern,
are two of these rules.

Three places lack a type or a symbol in a checked tree, and the verifier
takes each by its position. They are the callee of a call and the target
`c[i]` of an assignment on a value of a type parameter. The third is the
base of an enum value or of a function of a type. The name of a case in a variant arm has no
symbol and stands in that place alone. Expressions are shared in valid
trees, `std/anti/collection/map.antl` among them, so an expression may
stand twice. Refusing it failed the build of the standard library
(`s1-build-strict.log`).

M43. `damaged_trees` in `tests/unit/test_modules.c` writes the tree of a
generic with one of eighteen damages: out of range, NULL, self-holding,
shared, deeper than the limit and miscounted records. Before the fix the
reader accepted all eighteen, 36 failed checks (`s1-unit-before.log`).
After it each is refused and the undamaged tree and a short chain read
(`s1-unit-after.log`).

## Provisional entries

Both under "Libraries and runtime", after the entry on the tables:

- A tree nests at most 1024 records deep, `ANTL_TREE_DEPTH_MAX`. The
  deepest tree the parser allows is 512 records, 254 nested blocks.
- The rules a library file meets beyond its encoding stand in
  `antl_verify.c`. Steps 2 and 3 add to it.

## Left for later steps

- The writer still stores the lane count as one byte. A shuffle of 256
  lanes now writes a file the reader refuses instead of misreading it.
  Step 2 widens the count, S13.
- A left-nested chain of 2000 `+` builds a tree of more than 1024
  records, which antic writes and the reader now refuses. Step 4 bounds
  the tree the parser builds, S05.
- Lowering walks a shared expression once per path. A file of shared
  expressions nested 1024 deep can take exponential time. That is a
  hang, not a crash, and no rule bounds it yet.
- A generic whose body is `-` applied 100 times over a name did not
  compile in 20 seconds. The cause lies in the front end and is outside
  this step.
