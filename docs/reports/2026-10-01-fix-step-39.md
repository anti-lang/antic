# Fix step 39: standard `json` and `log`

Step 39 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` closes M29 and M09. Both
are fixed and committed. The step is BLOCKED before its push. The new
runtime code changes the executable that `link_identity_macos-arm64` pins.
Writing the new digest into `tests/link-identity/return42.macos-arm64.sha256`
was refused by the permission check of this session, so no suite is green.

## Findings

- M29, fixed in `7eee766c`. The runtime's scanner is now the only reader of
  JSON. `read_string` of `src/rt/json.c` feeds a sink that stores, counts or
  compares the bytes. `anti_rt_json_key` compares a key with its escapes read,
  and `anti_rt_json_member` refuses a text that is not a single object.
  `anti_rt_json_unquote` of `src/rt/object.c` reads a string twice, once to
  count it and once into the builder. The bytes before a fault stay in the
  builder, as the entry on `json.unquote` under "Standard library phase"
  decides. `put_text` is now `anti_rt_json_write_text`, and
  `json.write_text` binds it. `skip_space`, `end_of_text` and `end_of_value`
  are gone. `tests/std/json_strings.anti` failed on 20 lines before the fix.
- M09, fixed in `6d48b5e9`. `anti.log` imports `anti.fs`. The file sink uses
  `fs.open` with the new `Mode.Append`, then `fs.write` and the new
  `fs.flush`. The configuration file goes to TOML whole, read with
  `fs.read_file`. `tests/std/log.anti` showed the sliced path opening
  `log-slice.out-not-this`. `tests/std/log_nul.anti` gave `error` instead of
  `info` before the fix.
- A defect no finding names, fixed in `6d48b5e9`. A logger's name taken from
  the file pointed into the TOML document, which `from_configuration` freed on
  return. The test line with `name = "app"` printed freed bytes.
  `SinkLogger.new` now keeps a copy of the name.

## Provisional entries added

- `json.unquote`, `json.member` and `json.write_text` are bindings of the
  runtime. Space may stand around the string, and text after the closing
  quote is refused.
- `fs.Mode.Append`, `fs.flush` and the three `ANTI_FILE_*` modes of the
  platform layer.
- `anti.log` reads and writes through `anti.fs`, and `SinkLogger.new` copies
  its name.
