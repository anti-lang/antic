# Fix step 33, runtime platform layer, files and traces

Step 33 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M30 for `fs.c` and
`trace.c`. Logs are in `build/drive/logs/s33-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M30, `fs.c` and `trace.c` | Fixed. The 28 host `#if` lines of the two files are gone, and `trace.c` keeps no lock of its own. Its three locks are `ANTI_RT_LOCK_TRACE_TEXTS`, `ANTI_RT_LOCK_TRACE_FILES` and `ANTI_RT_LOCK_DEBUG`. `platform.h` gives the calls of files with a path in UTF-8, `anti_rt_trace_walk`, `anti_rt_module_at` and `anti_rt_debug_lookup`. |
| M30, `cpu.c`, `mem.c`, `start.c`, `errno.c`, `init.c` and `atomic.c` | Left for step 34, which names them. They stay on the list of `tests/run_platform_layer.cmake`. |

The minor rule 1 finding named `trace.c:137`, `trace.c:140` and
`trace.c:249`. The `#pragma comment`, the weak reference and
`__builtin_frame_address` now stand in the platform files, which the finding
names as their place. The rest of that finding belongs to step 34.

The Windows conversion of a path had two owners, `native_path` of `fs.c` and
`wide_of` of `platform_windows.c`. `wide_of` is the one now, and it sets
`errno`.

`anti_rt_debug_lookup` still reads the file name of `SymGetLineFromAddr64` in
the ANSI code page, as `trace.c` did. No finding names it, and it is
unchanged.

  change (`s33-red-unit.log`) and passes after it.

every target with the warning set (`s33-host-build2.log`). The VMs did not
run the unit tests.
