# Fix step 34, runtime platform layer, the rest

Step 34 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: M30 for `cpu.c`,
`mem.c`, `start.c`, `errno.c`, `init.c` and `atomic.c`, with the minor rule
1 finding on compiler extensions, `src/rt/lock.c:63` of `docs/audit/rt.md`.
Logs are in `build/drive/logs/s34-*`.

Status: green and pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| M30, `cpu.c`, `mem.c`, `start.c`, `init.c` | Fixed. Their host `#if` lines are gone. The layer gives `anti_rt_process_arguments`, `anti_rt_process_environment`, `anti_rt_streams_binary`, `anti_rt_aligned_alloc` and `anti_rt_aligned_free`, the empty `anti_rt_memory_kept`, `anti_rt_cpuid` and `anti_rt_xcr0` on x86_64 and `anti_rt_arm64_level` on ARM64. `start.c` has one `main` and names `anti.rt.main` through `ANTI_RT_ENTRY_LABEL` of `platform.h`. |
| M30, `errno.c` | Fixed before this step. Step 31 moved the text of a system error into the platform files, and the file held no host line at the start. It left the list of `tests/run_platform_layer.cmake` here. |
| M30, `atomic.c` | Fixed. The form follows the compiler: clang and gcc take the builtins on every target, and MSVC alone the Interlocked functions. The ARM64 barrier of MSVC reads `ANTI_RT_ARM64` of `platform.h`. |
| Minor, rule 1, extensions outside their files | Fixed. `__asm__` of `cpu.c` and `start.c` and the weak definition of `init.c` stand in the layer. `lock.c` and `snapshot.c` call the functions of `atomic.c` in place of their own `__atomic` builtins. |

The finding on `-isystem` for the PCRE2 glue, `CMakeLists.txt:124`, is a
second rule 1 row of the summary. Step 34 does not name it, so it is
unchanged.

`lock.c` and `snapshot.c` read and wrote with relaxed order before. The
functions of `atomic.c` are sequentially consistent, which is never wrong
where relaxed was right. The owner check of a hidden lock is now one call
more.

## Tests first

- `platform_layer` no longer exempts any runtime file. Before the change it
  failed on the six files (`s34-red-rule.log`), and after it passes.
- The new test `rt_extensions` refuses a compiler extension in `src/rt/`
  outside the platform layer and `atomic.c`. It failed on 14 lines of
  `cpu.c`, `init.c`, `lock.c`, `snapshot.c` and `start.c` before the
  change (`s34-red-ext.log`), and after it passes.
- `rt_platform` checks memory at every alignment up to 4096 and the
  arguments of the process: `one`, `two words` and one outside ASCII,
  passed by the test. It checks the repair of a byte that is no UTF-8 on
  macOS and Linux. It also checks a variable of the environment outside
  ASCII and CPUID or the ARM64 level, armv8.5 on this Mac. It did not
  compile before the change (`s34-red-unit.log`).
- The unit test of `test_link.c` that pins the spelling of the entry now
  reads `platform.h`, where the label stands, for all three formats.

The cross build compiles the runtime for every target and level with the
warning set (`s34-host-build1.log`). The objects for Windows name
`_A4anti2rt_main`, `GetCommandLineW` and `GetEnvironmentStringsW`, and those
for macOS name `_anti.rt.main` and `_NSGetEnviron`. The VMs did not run the
unit tests.

## Decisions

Two `[provisional]` entries were added beside the others of the layer in
`docs/decisions.md`:

- The start of a program, the memory at an alignment, the empty
  `anti_rt_memory_kept` and the processor queries stand on the platform
  layer. macOS reads the environment through `_NSGetEnviron`.
- `src/rt/atomic.c` chooses its form by the compiler, and `lock.c` and
  `snapshot.c` use its functions.

The entries on the raw-bytes rule, the Windows start and `LibcAllocator`
name the new place of the code, and so does `docs/notes/emitter.md`.
`tests/link-identity/return42.macos-arm64.sha256` holds the digest this
Mac gives, since the runtime changed.
