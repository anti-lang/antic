# The defect T207

The step closes T207, which the run of the documentation recorded on
2026-10-26. Logs stand in `build/drive/logs/defects/`.

## Fix

`7e39ac1f`. `delete(e)` of the error a `catch` binds freed the error, and
the handler deleted it a second time at its exit. A release build
stopped with a segmentation fault in `anti_rt_delete_from`.

`delete(e)` now moves the error, as a move into an `own ?*Error`
parameter does:

- `sema_move_caught` of `src/antic/sema_value.c` holds the checks of
  that move, which `sema_note_move` and `check_object` of
  `src/antic/sema_expr.c` share. A `delete` inside a loop of the handler,
  or while a `defer` or an `undo` of the handler names the error, is
  refused with the messages of the move. A use of the error after the
  `delete` is refused with `` `e` has moved into `delete` and is not
  named after it ``.
- The `EXPR_OBJECT` case of `src/antic/lower_expr.c` writes `none` into
  the handler's copy, so the delete at each exit of the handler passes
  over it.

No message is new: the three texts are those of the move into an `own`
parameter.

## The rule

No document said whether `delete(e)` is refused or moves the error. The
gap procedure gave a `[provisional]` entry after the entry on the move
into `own ?*Error` in `docs/decisions.md`. `docs/anti-object-model.md`,
the overview and chapter 6 of the guide say it in one sentence each.
