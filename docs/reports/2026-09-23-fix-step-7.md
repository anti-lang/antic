# Fix step 7: optimizer and register allocator

Step 7 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: S17, S18, M21 and M35 in
`src/antic/optimize.c` and `src/antic/regalloc.c`. Commits `8075a45` and
`866a100` on `fix/backend`, not pushed.

## Findings

| ID | Result |
|---|---|
| S17 | Fixed. `forward_stores` forgets the held store when an instruction writes its value, the base of its address or its offset. `address_of` treats an address as unknown once the base or the offset of its ptradd is written after it. `forward_after_write` in `tests/unit/test_optimize.c` failed in four of five cases before the fix. |
| S18 | Fixed. A read past the scratch registers borrows a register, see `docs/decisions-backend.md`. `tests/programs/spill_three.anti` printed `-9179492931621564644` on macos-arm64 before the fix and prints `43524940398` after it, as the x86_64 build did before. |
| M21 | Fixed. `same_operand` compares the member of the union that each operand kind writes, and replaces `same_offset` and the comparison inside `same_address`. `symbolic_offsets` in `test_optimize.c` failed for a split slot and for a parameter before the fix. |
| M35 | Fixed. The branch that cleared `definition[0]` goes, and a constant definition is dropped only when its uses got copies. The loop that ran once is straight code. `constant_in_register_0` in `tests/unit/test_regalloc.c` failed on macos-arm64 and linux-x86_64 before the fix. |

The docs checker also found three long sentences and one filler word
already in `regalloc.c`, which are rewritten.

## Gates

- Build: no compiler warnings. The link prints `ld: warning: ignoring
  -lto_library`, since `lib/libLTO.dylib` is missing under the pinned
  clang of `ANTI_DEPS_DIR`. The warning comes from the environment and
  not from this step, `build/drive/logs/s7-build.log`.
- Host: 791 of 795 pass, `build/drive/logs/s7-ctest-host.log`.
- ASan: 790 of 794 pass, `build/drive/logs/s7-ctest-asan.log`.
- UBSan: 790 of 794 pass, `build/drive/logs/s7-ctest-ubsan.log`.
- Docs checker: no finding on any touched file, `build/drive/logs/s7-docs.log`.

The four failures in each suite are `anti_bind_clang`, `anti_bind_raymath`,
`anti_bind_raylib` and `anti_bind_refusals`. `anti bind` looks for the
pinned clang in `deps/clang` beside the build tree (`find_clang` in
`src/anti/bindclang.c`). This worktree takes its downloads from
`ANTI_DEPS_DIR`, so `anti` falls back to `/usr/bin/clang`, which is clang
21, and refuses it: `anti: clang is clang 21, and anti bind was tested
against clang 23 only`. The four failed in the first run of the step,
after a change to `optimize.c` alone. `bindclang.c` lies outside this
step's fence.
