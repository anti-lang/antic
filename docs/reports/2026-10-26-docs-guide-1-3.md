## Questions answered from the code

- Does a search allocate? Yes. `anti_rt_regex_first`, `anti_rt_regex_test` and `anti_rt_regex_next` make the match data of PCRE2 and free it, and `pcre2_match` takes a frame vector of at least 20 KiB in it.
- Does a group of a match cost a search? Yes. `anti_rt_regex_group` and `anti_rt_regex_group_named` run the search again with room for every group.
- How much does PCRE2 add? 347,688 bytes of a release program for linux-x86_64: 576,456 against 228,768 bytes at `ed280bc0`.
- Is `&` on two `bool` values allowed? No. It needs integers, so `&&` is the one conjunction.
- Where do the per-function safety checks run? At the end of `sema_check_function`, and the lock graph after the copies of the generics.
