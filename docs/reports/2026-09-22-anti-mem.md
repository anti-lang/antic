# The module anti.mem and its Allocator

`anti.mem.Allocator` of "Standard interfaces" in `docs/anti-language-additions.md` is built,
with a default over the C library and an allocator of blocks. The development Mac passes 639
of 639 tests, and the ASan and UBSan builds pass 638 each, without `no_paths`. The build has
no warnings. The VMs did not run.

## What was done

1. `e76b657`. `alloc` and `free` are keywords, and the work order names the two functions of
   `Allocator` so. The parser now reads either as the name of a struct or class function,
   after `fn` and after the `::` of a qualified `concrete fn`, and as a member after `.`.
   Everywhere else both stay keywords. `programs/member_keywords.anti` declares both
   abstract, fills them, one qualified, and calls them through the table, directly and as a
   bound function, with the built-in `alloc` and `free` inside. `errors/member_keywords.anti`
   keeps a module function, a field, a parameter and a local of either name refused.
2. `18f160f`. `std/anti/mem.anti` holds the abstract class `Allocator` with
   `alloc(self, size: int, align: int) -> ?*byte` and `free(self, p: ?*byte)`. The singleton
   `LibcAllocator` is the default, whose `get` a provider names. `rt/mem.c` gives any power
   of two with `posix_memalign`, and with `_aligned_malloc` on Windows, whose memory only
   `_aligned_free` gives back. `ArenaAllocator.new(from, block)` takes blocks from another
   allocator, hands out memory from them, and gives all of them back in `free_all` and in
   `destruct`. The language's `alloc` and `free` are unchanged.
3. The tests. `std/mem.anti` takes the default through every alignment from 1 to 4096 and the
   requests it refuses. `std/mem_arena.anti` lends the blocks through a counting allocator,
   checks that small requests share blocks without overlap, that `free` gives nothing back
   and that `free_all` and `destruct` give every block back. `std/mem_class.anti` runs a
   class that takes an `Allocator` over the default, over the counting allocator, which sees
   each block of its growth come back, and over an `ArenaAllocator`.
4. `1bfce8f` drops a comparison with `SIZE_MAX` that no 64-bit target can meet, and
   `f3e8d2d` records the module and the counts in `CLAUDE.md`. The status line under
   "Injection" in `docs/anti-syntax-overview.md` names what is built and what is not.

## What failed and how it was fixed

- `emit_identity` failed on the new program `member_keywords.anti`, whose assembly had no
  digest. The manifest was written on the Mac with `-DWRITE=yes`. It gained six lines and
  lost none, so the parser change moved no other output.
- `link_identity_macos-arm64` failed, since the build id covers the runtime library and
  `rt/mem.c` is new in it. Two runs gave one digest, which replaced the old one.
- The checker bans the name of the allocator of blocks as a bare word. The comments and
  entries name `ArenaAllocator` in code or say "the allocator" instead.
- A mutation of each part was caught: `free_all` that keeps its blocks fails
  `std_mem_arena` and `std_mem_class`, and `malloc` in place of `posix_memalign` fails
  `std_mem`.
- The three programs also link for the five other targets, and the `macos-x86_64` builds at
  `--cpu v1` pass under Rosetta.

## Provisional entries

- "Lexical rules": `alloc` and `free` as member names.
- "`anti.mem`": the signature of `Allocator`, `none` for a bad request and a size of zero,
  `LibcAllocator` as the singleton default over `rt/mem.c`, and the block policy of
  `ArenaAllocator`.
