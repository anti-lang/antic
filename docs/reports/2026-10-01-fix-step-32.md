## Decisions

The entry on `enum anti_rt_lock` under "Repository layout" in
`docs/decisions.md` now names the six locks and the monitor. Three
`[provisional]` entries were added beside it:

- the word of a Mutex is `struct anti_rt_word` of `platform.h`, and the
  orders of a dev build take `ANTI_RT_LOCK_ORDERS`;
- the layer starts a thread and counts the processors. The pool starts
  its workers under its lock at the first call, in place of a once of the
  system;
- the route of a signal, the self-pipe or the console handler, stands in
  the layer, and so does the image of an address.

`tests/link-identity/return42.macos-arm64.sha256` holds the digest this
Mac gives, since the runtime changed.
