# anti.graphics

The session built `anti.graphics` as Eddie approved it on 2026-11-06, over
raylib and `anti.math`, and rewrote a copy of the Pythagoras tree on it.

## What changed

- `0a1eaecd` builds the module.
  - `src/std/anti/graphics.anti` holds the window, the canvas, `Say`,
    `Texture`, `Font`, the colours of raylib, `Key`, `Mouse`, `Pad`, the
    interface `Display`, its default `RaylibDisplay` and the `Recorder`.
    `src/native/raylib.cmake` compiles it after `anti.raylib` into
    `std/anti/` of the runtime archive. A program needs no manifest entry
    and links raylib through `anti.raylib`.
  - The unit square stands on `Camera2D`. `BeginMode2D` of raylib 6.0
    works in the pixels of the framebuffer, so the zoom is the shorter side
    of the render size, as the note says. A canvas holds the product of its
    moves, turns and scales, laid around each shape with `rlPushMatrix`,
    `rlMultMatrixf` and `rlPopMatrix`.
  - `anti_rt_main_thread` of `src/rt/init.c` marks the thread of the first
    `anti_rt_init`. `Window.start` fails with `NotMainThread` on any
    other thread, before the display.
  - The note now writes `graphics.hex`, `graphics.hsv`, `.text()` at the
    end of a `say` chain and a `tests` block that compiles. Its sketch of
    the tree was right and stays as it was.
- `fc0f5d58` records T209 and T210, two defects of antic the step met.

## Tests

- `std_graphics_tree` draws the sketch of the note through `Recorder.new`
  at 30, 45 and 60 degrees. Each child stands on its part of the top edge
  of its parent, the legs are `cos a` and `sin a` long, they meet at a
  right angle and stand outside the parent.
- `std_graphics_colors`: the 26 named colours are raylib's, and `hex`,
  `hsv`, `fade`, `lerp` and `brightness` give what raylib gives.
- `std_graphics_recorder` runs three frames of a window over the
  Recorder that `--inject` gives. They draw every shape on a turned and
  scaled canvas, with a texture and a font, and read the input and the
  clock.
- `std_graphics_thread`: `open` in a job of the pool fails with
  `NotMainThread`, and on the main thread it opens.
- Each of the four runs in release mode, in dev mode and with
  `--memory-checks`.
- `std_graphics_allocations_memory_checks`: a hook of AddressSanitizer
  counts every allocation of the process across 1000 frames. The frames
  draw every shape and a tree of six levels, write a score through `say`
  and read the input and the clock. It counts 0, and 1000 with one
  `f"..."` per frame.
- `inject_graphics_dev` and `inject_graphics_release` run `anti test` on a
  project whose `[inject.test]` names `anti.graphics.Recorder.shared`.
  One module holds the sketch and the `tests` block of the note, and one
  draws in a window over the Recorder.
- `graphics_link_<target>` links the first program of the note and the
  tree for all six targets.
- `graphics_note_examples` finds every `anti` block of the note word for
  word in a program the suite builds. It failed on the `tests` block of
  the note before the note was fixed.
- `graphics_signatures` refuses a public item of the module that names
  `rl`.
- `matrix_classes_function_named_init` and
  `matrix_modules_function_of_a_constant_named_with_its_module` are the
  known failures of T209 and T210, in three modes each.

## By hand on the Mac

The suite opens no window. `tests/std/graphics_hello.anti` ran on the Mac
for four seconds. A screenshot showed a window of 1706 by 960 points, two
thirds of the monitor of 2560 by 1440, centred. A round pink circle of
half its height stood in its centre. The first build took the zoom from
the screen size and drew the circle at half its size. The zoom is the
render size for that reason. The release build of the rewritten tree ran
for five seconds. It showed the swaying tree from pink to green on its
night sky, with the glow behind the crown.

## Acceptance

`build/pythagoras-tree` of this checkout is `git archive` of
`pythagoras-tree` at `204489d`, which stays untouched.

- `main.anti` imports `anti.graphics` and no `anti.raylib`. It went from
  113 lines to 100.
- `geometry.anti` imports `anti.math` in place of the `extern fn` of
  `sinf` and `cosf`, and keeps its 18 tests. The layout is computed in
  units of the visible rectangle, `f.bounds`, and drawn on the canvas
  moved to its corner.
- `tuning.anti` keeps every number but `WIDTH` and `HEIGHT`, since a
  program of `anti.graphics` names no pixel size. `OUTLINE_WIDTH` stays in
  pixels and becomes units through `f.pixel`.
- `anti build` and `anti build --release` build it from an empty
  `anti.toml`. `anti test -I src -I build/macos-arm64/dev/lib
  src/tree/geometry.anti` passes its 18 tests in dev and release mode, as
  `build.sh` of the original passes its library directory.

## Provisional entries

Under "Standard interfaces" of `docs/decisions.md`:

- `graphics.hex` and `graphics.hsv` as functions of the module.
- `say()` with `add`, `add_int`, `add_float` and `text()`.
- `Window.start`, and one window at a time.
- The window opened hidden, sized to two thirds of the monitor, centred.
- The camera in the render size, `f.pixel`, and the mouse and touch scaled.
- `f.bounds` of the frame, and `f.pixel` that follows `scale`.
- The product of a canvas laid on the matrix of rlgl per shape.
- The segments of a circle by its radius in pixels.
- The input, timing and window functions, and `Pad.A` to `Pad.Y`.
- `Texture`, `Font`, `text_ex` and the spacing of a text.
- The form of the Recorder and of its provider.
- `anti_rt_main_thread`.
- The count of allocations through the hooks of AddressSanitizer.

## Questions for Eddie

1. The note wrote `Color.hex` and `Color.hsv`, and a struct holds fields
   alone. They are `graphics.hex` and `graphics.hsv` now. Should a struct
   take static functions, or do the two stay functions of the module?
2. `f.text` takes a `str`, so a `say` chain ends with `.text()`. Is that
   the form, or should `f.text` take the `Say` of the window as well?
3. T210 refuses `graphics.PINK.fade(0.3)`, while `PINK.fade(0.3)`
   compiles. A program that imports the module plainly meets it at its
   first colour. Should its fix come before the next step of the module?
4. `anti test` of a project finds the library files of its own modules
   through `-I` alone, so the tree passes the directory that `anti build`
   wrote. Should `anti test` read `anti.toml` and find them itself?
