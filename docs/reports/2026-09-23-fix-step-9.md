# Fix step 9: COFF

Step 9 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md` closes S19, M8 and M28
in `src/antic/coff.c`. Each fix began with a test in
`tests/unit/test_coff.c` that failed.

## Findings

| ID | Result | Test that failed first |
|---|---|---|
| S19 | fixed | `test_uninitialised` crashed the unit binary with SIGBUS |
| M8 | fixed | `test_long_names` accepted `/ 4`, `/+4`, `/4x` and `/4 ` |
| M28 | fixed | `test_section_limit` accepted a join of 0xFF00 sections |

- S19. `read_sections` passes over a section marked uninitialised, as the
  writer does. The test marks a `.drectve` and a `.debug$S` uninitialised,
  with a raw pointer and size of 0xFFFFFFF0.
- M8. The 7 bytes after the slash are read as decimal digits up to the
  first NUL, and any other byte is refused. `strtoul` is gone. The test
  also checks that `/0000004` is still read, and that an empty name and
  `0x4` are refused.
- M28. A join of more than 0xFEFF sections, `IMAGE_SYM_SECTION_MAX`, is
  refused with an error. The same bound keeps every section number of a
  symbol and of an associative record below the reserved -2 and -1. The
  test joins 0xFEFF sections and then 0xFF00.

## Decisions

Two `[provisional]` entries under "COFF join" in
`docs/decisions-backend.md`: the pass over an uninitialised section, and
the end of a long name at its first NUL.
