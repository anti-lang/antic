# Step abi of the LLVM back end

Step `abi` of `docs/work-order-llvm-back-end.md`: the section "Calling convention".
Commits `9bcfaac9` and `5555ef27`. The native back end is unchanged.

## What the step built

- `src/antic/abi.h` with `enum abi_kind` and `struct abi_param` in the form of the
  section, and `abi_classify(t, l, f, params, result)`.
- `src/antic/abi.c`, 334 lines. It classifies the parameters and the result of a
  function for the five conventions of `target_info`. System V classifies each
  eightbyte as `i64`, `double`, `<2 x float>` or `float`, makes a MEMORY aggregate
  `byval` and a MEMORY result `sret`, and counts the registers as `locate` does.
  The three AAPCS64 conventions take `[N x float]` or `[N x double]` for a
  homogeneous float aggregate, `[N x i64]` or `i128` for another one of at most 16
  bytes, and a pointer to a copy or `sret` above it. Windows x64 takes an integer
  of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bytes and a pointer to a copy or `sret` for any other size. A
  simd struct of 16 bytes is `ABI_VECTOR`, except a parameter of Windows x64, which
  is a pointer to a copy.
- Nothing calls `abi_classify` yet. The step `emit-memory` does.

## What it tested

- `tests/unit/test_abi.c`, the test `unit_abi`, holds nine cases. They cover the
  scalars of all six targets and the eightbytes, the MEMORY class and the
  register count of System V. They also cover the vectors, the AAPCS64 classes on
  its three conventions, its block allocation, the variadic rule of Windows ARM64
  and Windows x64. The
  expectations come from the psABI, the AAPCS64, Apple's arm64 document and
  Microsoft's two convention documents.
- Red first: with a stub that only cleared the output, the test failed 146
  checks (`build/drive/logs/llvm-abi-red-run.log`). With the System V register
  count at 7 and the `i128` rule off, it failed 6
  (`build/drive/logs/llvm-abi-mut.log`).
- The three suites at `5555ef27`, each with `-j14`: `host` 1470 of 1470 passed
  (`build/drive/logs/llvm-abi-host.log`), `asan` 1469 of 1469
  (`build/drive/logs/llvm-abi-asan.log`), `ubsan` 1469 of 1469
  (`build/drive/logs/llvm-abi-ubsan.log`). The builds had no warning.
  `emit_identity` and `link_identity_macos-arm64` passed unchanged.

## Provisional entries

All four stand in `docs/decisions.md` after the entry on homogeneous float
aggregates.

- `abi_classify` takes the layouts of the module beside the target.
- An AAPCS64 aggregate of at most 16 bytes aligned to 16 is `i128`, so LLVM starts
  it at an even register as rule C.8 asks.
- System V counts its registers and makes an aggregate that no longer fits
  `byval`.
- On Windows ARM64 a named aggregate of a variadic function is never a float
  aggregate or a vector. `locate` of `arm64.c` differs here, so the two back ends
  disagree on that one case until the step `switch`.
