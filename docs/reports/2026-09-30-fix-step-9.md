# Fix step 9: antic lowering of loops, and the IR printer

Step 9 of "Fix steps" in `docs/audit/summary.md`: S20 and S22, with the
19 pinned outputs rewritten. The detail stands in
`docs/audit/back-end.md`. The logs lie in `build/drive/logs/s9-*.log`.

## Findings

| Id | Result |
|---|---|
| S20 | Fixed, `1cf4e6b` |
| S22 | Fixed, `1737305` |

S20. The counter of a range now holds the value of the turn, and the
step measures the distance to the bound that ends the walk before it
moves: `high - counter` upward and `counter - low` downward, compared
unsigned with k. Both are differences of two values of the range, so
neither wraps. An empty range is one comparison by the sign of the type
before the first turn, and the first value of `by -k` divides unsigned.
The checker refuses a step that does not fit the type of the range, so k
fits the unsigned type of the width. The bounds are copied before the
loop, since the step reads them again. `programs/by_ends.anti` walks to
both ends of `u8`, `i8`, `i16`, `int` and `u64`, with constant bounds
and bounds read at run time. Before the fix it printed `runaway` for
eight of its loops, nothing for five that have values, and 27 values for
the empty `100 as i8..-100 by -50` (`s9-byends1.log`). It passes on
macos-arm64 and macos-x86_64.

S22. `ir_name_append` in `ir_print.c` writes `module.name`, or the name
alone when there is no module, and every text that names a function or
a global goes through it: the printer, the verifier, the machine printer
and the two errors of the register allocator. `no_module` of
`test_ir.c` pins both forms, and failed with `(null).` before the fix
(`s9-unit1.log`). The 19 pinned files read `global anti_rt_slots` where
they read `global (null).anti_rt_slots`.

`tests/emit-identity/programs.sha256` was written again for the 34
programs with a range, 204 files.
