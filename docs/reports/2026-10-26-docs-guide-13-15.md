## Questions answered from the code

- Does a release build take a count of chunks below zero? Yes, as zero, the count of the machine, in `anti_rt_parallel`.
- Is `parallel` a wait of C5? No. `enum wait_kind` holds `recv`, `send`, `select`, `join` and `join_all`.
- Does a private function of a synchronized class that waits get the warning at its call? Yes, as `build/g1315/p/c5b.anti` shows.
- Does a closure that `dispatch` passes on compile with a `delete`? Yes. `sema_walk_worker` follows calls by name and enters no closure.
- Which operations does an atomic `bool`, pointer and `f16` take? Those of the table of chapter 13, from `sema_atomic_call`.
