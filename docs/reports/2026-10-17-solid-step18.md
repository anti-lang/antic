# Solid: step 18, the headers and the platform layer of the runtime

The step fixed the findings of step 18 of "Fix steps" in
`docs/audit/summary.md`: M321, M337, M316 and M317, with the minor
finding under rule 22. It took decisions 6, 7 and 14 of "Decisions of
2026-10-11". It ran in one session on 2026-10-17. The logs lie in
`build/drive/logs/`, under names that start with `s18-`.

`5c4f9fe8` writes the commits into the summary, and `0c63323c` writes
the digest of `link_identity_macos-arm64` again.

- Decision 14. `rt_extensions` refuses `_MSC_VER`, the Interlocked
  intrinsics, `<intrin.h>`, `_M_X64` and `_M_ARM64` in every file of
  `src/rt/`, and failed on `atomic.c` before the change,
  `s18-red-ext.log`.
- M321. `str.h` holds `struct anti_text`, which seven headers include in
  place of all of `std.h`. `fail.h` holds the failure routines of
  `assert.c`, `check.c` and `cast.c`. `platform.h` declares the seven
  functions the platform files defined from `std.h` and `rt.h`, `hash.h`
  the four of `hash.c`, and `rt.h` opens with its purpose. The new test
  `rt_headers` holds each of those files to its header, and failed
  before, `s18-red-headers.log`.
- M317. `anti_rt_path_list_separator` of the layer gives `;` on Windows.
  Discovery splits at it, the configuration joins an array with it, and
  `--anti.help` shows `<dir;dir>` there. `rt_plugin_table` keeps the
  drive of its directory and finds the library in the second directory
  of a list. On anti-windows the drive and the second directory failed
  with the split at `:`, `s18-win-red2.log`. `conf_help` and
  `conf_array` take a `.win32.pattern` on Windows.
- M337. `find_start`, `find_next` and `find_end` are the one walk. `C:`
  is the current directory of the drive, a name that does not fit ends
  the walk with ENAMETOOLONG, and a file gives ENOTDIR on every system.
- M316. A file name keeps what the system holds. Windows writes a
  surrogate alone as the three bytes of its value, `anti_rt_name_bytes`
  and `anti_rt_name_units` of `utf.c`, which `unit_utf` checks on every
  host. `rt_platform` makes such a name through the system, lists it and
  opens it by the listed name on Linux and Windows, and expects EILSEQ
  from macOS. `src/rt/platform.h`, `anti.fs`, the overview and chapter 3
  of the guide state the exception.
- Rule 22. `rt_platform` walks from 100 frames down, and with a skip
  below zero. On anti-windows six checks failed before the fixes,
  `s18-win-red2.log`. They were the deep walk, the room of 70, the
  negative skip, the listing of the drive, ENOTDIR and the kept name.
  They pass after the fixes, `s18-win-green1.log`.
