## What changed

- `docs/anti-language-additions.md` takes every section of round five. "Generics" keeps
  its six subsections. "Optional values", "Ownership at a call", "Lending", "Walking a
  collection", "Hashing and order" and "Direct imports" follow. "Collections" holds the
  module table as "Modules", and "Thread-safe collections", "Shared ownership" and
  "Memory checks" close it.
  The note on the word `lent` joins "Lending". The round-five messages join "Messages".
- The changes its "What to change" section lists: "Timing" places round five after
  round four, with generics first. "Keywords" adds `constraint`, `type` and the
  contextual `lent`, and the tokens of type arguments, `?T` and `.{`. The table of
  language hooks gains `hash`. "Constraints" ships `Number` and `Ordered` in
  `anti.lang`. "Nullable pointers" and "Iteration" now point at `?T`, `for x in &c`
  and `Iterable<T>`, where they said `Iterable[T]`.
- `docs/anti-syntax-overview.md` gains "Generics", "Optional values", "Direct imports"
  and "Collections", each with an `anti not-built` example and a status line. "Types",
  "Loops", "Ownership", "Operators on classes", "Checks and debugging", "Standard
  library", "Later" and "Reserved words" name the round-five features and mark them not
  built. The reserved-words line no longer calls the prefix `re` unbuilt, which it is.
- `docs/anti-object-model.md`: the hook `hash` in "Operators", generics in "Not in the
  language" point at the additions, and "Keywords" names round five.
- `docs/tooling-addendum.md`: `--memory-checks` for `anti build`, `run` and `test`, and
  the sorted names of a direct import in the formatter rules.
- `docs/decisions.md`: a section "Generics and collections" records round five and its
  status. The provisional refusal of `for x in &e` over a collection and the `anti.mem`
  entry on containers now name the round-five rule that replaces them once built.
- `CLAUDE.md` says round five stands in the additions and is not built.
- `docs/anti-language-additions-5.md` is deleted.

No `[provisional]` decision was made.
