- What does a release build do with a held `Mutex`? `sync_held` ended with signal 9 on macOS.
- What does a release build do with a second `join`? `join_twice` waited until the 10 seconds of the probe ran out.
- Does the work directory of `anti test` count as a root? Yes, first, by `test_run` of `src/anti/test.c`.
- Do the bench programs hold assertions? No, and the library code they reach holds them in `tests` blocks alone.
