# Solid: step 11, the lexer, the parser and the pattern reader of antic

The step fixed the findings of step 11 of "Fix steps" in
`docs/audit/summary.md`: S303, M306, M307 and M308, with the two
sentences of S320 in `lexer.h`. It ran in one session on 2026-10-16. The
logs lie in `build/drive/logs/`, under names that start with `s11-`.

## Findings closed

| Finding | Commit | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| M306 | `dd7cce0d` | `placeholder` of `lexer.c` lexed an `{expr}` on a copy of the lexer and never copied its count of errors back, so the errors of placeholders escaped `LEX_ERRORS_MAX`. |
| S320, two sentences | `dd7cce0d` | The two comments of `lexer.h` on the cap said the errors of a literal count toward it and that every error is reported. |
| M308 | `b1e47d47` | `placeholder` of `parser_expr.c` copied back `ok` alone, so the reach of the tree of an `{expr}` was lost to the chain around the literal. |
| M307 | `68fd6aa2`, `1d0804e0` | The loop was gone already, closed as T103. A block at the depth limit still gave the message once per statement, and twice for a `do`. |
| S303 | `ffeda0a3` | The pattern reader counted in `long` and added each part of a sequence before it capped the sum, which overflows where a `long` is 32 bits. |

`6e81bb80` writes the commits into the summary.

- M306. The count comes back, the placeholder stops past the cap, and
  the rest of the literal is skipped. Two cases of `error_cap` in
  `test_lexer.c`, a run of 33332 placeholders `{@}` and two placeholders
  of 300 bad bytes followed by source, gave more than 101 diagnostics
  before the fix, `s11-unit1v.log`.
- M308. The case of `depth_limit` in `test_parser.c` puts a chain of 200
  links in an `{expr}` and one of 200 around the literal. The parser
  took it before the fix, `s11-unit1v.log`, and refuses it after.
- M307. The audit's input, 256 nested blocks before `let x = 1`, gives
  one message at `68fd6aa2`. Each word that stops the recovery, at the
  five depths past the limit, now has a case. `do { } while true;` and
  three statements in one block gave two and three messages,
  `s11-unit8v.log`. A block whose statements are all refused by the
  limit now reports once and is skipped to its `}`.
- S303. The new unit test `unit_pattern` reads the audit's trigger with
  `pattern_least_bytes`. On the Mac a `long` is 64 bits and it passed
  before the fix. On anti-windows it failed with two checks before the
  fix, `s11-win-before.log`, and passed after it, `s11-win-after.log`.
  PCRE2 refuses every pattern that reaches the cap as too large, so no
  program of antic reached the overflow. The audit had not run that.
